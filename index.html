Questions

What is Saga Labs?

Saga Labs is an independent AI engineering studio founded in 2023. It designs and ships its own products across iOS, web and agent infrastructure, and takes on a small number of external engagements each quarter. Seven apps are live on the App Store.

What is Dora?

Dora is Saga Labs' AI video and image generator. You upload a photo or write a prompt and get a finished clip back in about two minutes. It runs on the web at doravideo.com, on iPhone, and from inside AI assistants including Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and Google Antigravity. One account and one balance across all of them.

Did Saga Labs build the Dora MCP server?

Yes. Dora MCP is a hosted Model Context Protocol server built and operated by Saga Labs. One OAuth-secured endpoint at doravideo.com/mcp gives a connected agent four tools and sixteen models, each with its cost listed before generating. It is in the official MCP Registry, Smithery and the Cursor Directory. The full specification, limitations included, is published at doravideo.com/compare.

Does Saga Labs take on outside projects?

Yes, selectively. We are an app studio, so the work we take on looks like the work we do for ourselves: designing and shipping iOS and web apps, wiring generative AI into them, and the unglamorous production parts — payments, cost control, rate limiting, abuse defence, observability. Briefs of two to twelve weeks. Email sagalabs@proton.me.

Does Pinku Private AI work offline?

Yes. Pinku runs entirely on-device using models like Llama, Qwen and DeepSeek. It needs no internet connection, collects no data, and keeps every conversation on your iPhone.
