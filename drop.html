Drop

Calming sounds that keep playing with the screen locked, and a breathing guide for winding down or refocusing.

Free to download, with ten sounds free and no ads. Drop Pro unlocks the rest.

What it does

  • 32 sounds, ten freeNature, ambient and noise sounds. Free: rain, dewdrops, fire, forest, river, coffee shop, ocean waves, thunderstorm, bus and fan.
  • Plays with the screen lockedStart a sound and lock the phone; it keeps playing.
  • Breathing guideChoose how long each breath lasts, from 2 to 8 seconds, and how many cycles, then follow the animation.
  • A plant that growsEach session grows a plant through five stages, with streaks and achievements, so the habit is easier to keep.
  • WidgetsStart a session from the home screen.
  • No ads, no account, offlineNothing to sign up for, and it works without a connection.
  • iPhone, iPad and MacThe same app on all three.

What it does not do

Drop has a sleep timer (15 minutes to 8 hours, 30-second fade-out) but no alarm. It plays one sound at a time, with no mixer, and has no generated white, pink or brown noise. It has no sleep stories, guided voice meditations or sleep tracking. Questions

Is Drop free?

It is free to download, with ten sounds free and no ads: rain, dewdrops, fire, forest, river, coffee shop, ocean waves, thunderstorm, bus and fan. Drop Pro unlocks the other 22 sounds, by subscription or with a one-time lifetime purchase.

Does Drop keep playing with the screen locked?

Yes. The sound keeps playing with the screen locked, so the phone can stay dark on the nightstand.

Does Drop have a sleep timer?

Yes, since version 2.8. Tap Timer on the sound screen and pick 15, 30 or 45 minutes, 1, 2 or 8 hours; the sound fades out over 30 seconds and stops. Or choose no timer and it plays until you stop it.

Can I mix sounds?

No. Drop plays one sound at a time. If you want to layer rain and a fan, apps such as Rain Rain, Dark Noise or myNoise have a mixer.

Does Drop have white, pink or brown noise?

Not as generated noise. Its noise sounds are a fan, a bus and an electric hum.

Do I need an account? Does it work offline?

No account, and it works offline.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Drop is made by Saga Labs, an independent app studio.
