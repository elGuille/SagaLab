What it does not do

Drop has a sleep timer (15 minutes to 8 hours, 30-second fade-out) but no alarm. It plays one sound at a time, with no mixer or equaliser. It has no sleep stories, guided voice meditations or sleep tracking. Questions

Is Drop free?

It is free to download, with nine sounds free and no ads: white noise, a crackling fire, a forest, a heartbeat, a lake, a coffee shop, falling snow, desert wind and dolphins. Drop Pro unlocks the other 44 sounds, including rain, ocean waves and pink and brown noise,, by subscription or with a one-time lifetime purchase.

Does Drop keep playing with the screen locked?

Yes. The sound keeps playing with the screen locked, so the phone can stay dark on the nightstand. Since version 2.11 (24 October 2026) it also resumes after a phone call, stops when you unplug your headphones instead of playing out loud, and no longer starts by itself in the morning when the phone connects to the car or to AirPods.

Does Drop have a sleep timer?

Yes, since version 2.8. Tap Timer on the sound screen and pick 15, 30 or 45 minutes, 1, 2 or 8 hours; the sound fades out over 30 seconds and stops. Or choose no timer and it plays until you stop it.

What is Ask Drop?

Since version 2.9, on iPhones with Apple Intelligence, there is a box at the top of the sounds screen. Type what you need ("something to sleep to with rain, 1 hour" or "coffee shop sounds to focus for 45 minutes") and Drop proposes one of its sounds and a sleep timer; you tap Play. It uses Apple's on-device model, so the request stays on the phone. It only picks sounds Drop has (and, without Drop Pro, only free ones). iPhones without Apple Intelligence do not show the box.

Can I mix sounds?

No. Drop plays one sound at a time. If you want to layer rain and a fan, apps such as Rain Rain, Dark Noise or myNoise have a mixer.

Does Drop have white, pink or brown noise?

Yes, since version 2.10 (24 October 2026): white noise is free, and pink, brown and deep brown noise come with Drop Pro. They are looped tracks played one at a time, with no mixer or equaliser to change the colour.

Do I need an account? Does it work offline?

No account, and it works offline.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone, iPad and Mac. On the iPhone and iPad there are 53 sounds (nine free), and since version 2.11 the player fills the screen on the iPad and on an unfolded iPhone Duo. The Mac app has 32 sounds (ten free).

Do Drop's sounds loop without a gap?

Yes, since version 2.11 (24 October 2026). The 23 looping sounds were re-encoded and the join is crossfaded over 2 seconds, so there is no silence or click when a sound repeats, also with the screen locked.

Drop is made by Saga Labs, an independent app studio.
