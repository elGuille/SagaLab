How Pinku decides what your device can run

Pinku lists a model only if its download is at most 60% of the device's memory. That is why the table says, for example, that Qwen3 8B (4.7 GB) needs 7.9 GB.

Before loading a model, the iPhone and iPad app also checks how much memory is free at that moment, so a listed model can still be refused if other apps are using memory. Closing them helps.

Pinku needs iOS or iPadOS 18.6 or later and a device with Metal 3, which Apple lists for iPhone 12 or later, iPad Pro 11-inch (3rd generation), iPad Pro 12.9-inch (5th generation), iPad Air (4th generation), iPad mini (6th generation), iPad (9th generation) or later. On a Mac it needs Apple silicon and macOS 14 or later. The iPhone 11 and the iPhone SE (2nd generation) cannot run it.

Questions people ask

Can my iPhone run Qwen3 8B?

Pinku lists Qwen3 8B (4.7 GB) when the device has at least 7.9 GB of memory. That means 12 GB iPhones (iPhone 17 Pro, iPhone Air, iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone Duo). On 8 GB iPhones it is right at the limit and may not be listed.

What is the best model for an iPhone with 4 GB of memory?

On 4 GB iPhones (iPhone 12, 12 mini, 13, 13 mini, SE 3rd generation) Pinku lists models up to about 2.2 GB: Gemma 3 1B, Llama 3.2 1B and 3B, Qwen3 0.6B and 1.7B, LFM2.5 1.2B and SmolLM3 3B. Qwen3 4B is at the limit.

Do these models work without internet?

Yes. You download a model once inside Pinku; after that every answer is generated on the device, in airplane mode too.

Why does my iPhone not show a model this tool says fits?

The device reports slightly less memory than its advertised size, and Pinku also checks free memory before loading. Models marked at the limit can go either way.
