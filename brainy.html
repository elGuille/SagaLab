Brainy

Chat with AI, make images from a sentence, and scan text or a math problem with your camera. One app on your iPhone.

Free to try. Subscription: $6.99 a week or $89.99 a year (US App Store).

Download on the App Store

What it does

  • AI chatAsk questions, get help with writing, rewrite a text, study a topic. Answers come from DeepSeek.
  • Images from textDescribe what you want and Brainy draws it, with Google's Nano Banana model (Gemini 2.5 Flash Image).
  • Camera scannerPoint the camera at a math problem, a formula or a page of text. Brainy reads the text into the chat, so you can ask for an explanation without typing it.
  • In your languageThe interface is in English, Spanish, Japanese and Korean.

What it does not do

Brainy chats with one model, DeepSeek; you cannot pick GPT, Claude or Gemini for chat (our app Fast AI does that). It makes images, not videos (for video, Dora). It does not read PDFs or other files. Free use has daily limits, and the subscription raises them.

Questions

Which AI does Brainy use?

Chat answers come from DeepSeek. Images are made with Google's Nano Banana model (Gemini 2.5 Flash Image). Both are reached through OpenRouter.

Is Brainy free?

You can download it and try chat and images for free, with daily limits. The subscription, $6.99 a week or $89.99 a year on the US App Store, raises them.

Can Brainy solve math homework from a photo?

It helps. The camera reads the problem into the chat, and you ask Brainy to explain or solve it. Check the result: AI makes mistakes, especially with long calculations.

Does Brainy make videos?

No, only images. For AI video, our app Dora turns a photo or a description into a video.

Can I choose GPT, Claude or Gemini for chat?

No. Brainy chats with DeepSeek only. If you want to switch between models from several companies, our app Fast AI does that.

Brainy is made by Saga Labs, an independent app studio.
