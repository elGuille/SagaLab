Pio

Chat with AI, make images from a sentence, and scan text or a math problem with your camera. One app on your iPhone.

Free to try. Subscription: $6.99 a week or $49.99 a year (US App Store).

Download on the App Store

What it does

  • AI chatAsk questions, get help with writing, rewrite a text, study a topic. Answers come from Google Gemini.
  • Images and videos from textDescribe what you want and Pio makes it. You choose the model; each one shows its price in credits. Needs the subscription or credits.
  • Camera scannerPoint the camera at a math problem, a formula or a page of text. Pio reads the text into the chat, so you can ask for an explanation without typing it.
  • In your languageThe interface is in English, Spanish, Japanese and Korean.

What it does not do

Pio chats with one model, Google Gemini; you cannot pick GPT, Claude or another model for chat (our app Ask AI does that). Images and videos are not free: they need the subscription or credits (for an app made only for video, Dora). It does not read PDFs or other files. Free chat has daily limits, and the subscription raises them.

Questions

Which AI does Pio use?

Since version 1.5 (25 October 2026), chat answers come from Google Gemini (Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite). Images and videos are made with several image and video models, which the app lists with their price in credits.

Is Pio free?

You can download it and try the chat for free, with daily limits. Making images and videos needs the subscription or credits. The subscription, $6.99 a week or $49.99 a year on the US App Store, raises the chat limits.

Can Pio solve math homework from a photo?

It helps. The camera reads the problem into the chat, and you ask Pio to explain or solve it. Check the result: AI makes mistakes, especially with long calculations.

Does Pio make videos?

Yes, since version 1.5, with the subscription and credits. For an app made only for AI video, our app Dora turns a photo or a description into a video.

Can I choose GPT, Claude or Gemini for chat?

No. Pio picks the chat model for you (Google Gemini since version 1.5). If you want to switch between models from several companies, our app Ask AI does that.

Pio is made by Saga Labs, an independent app studio.
