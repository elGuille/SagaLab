How it works

Press the key or the microphone

On iPhone, switch to the Speakboard keyboard and tap the microphone. On Mac, hold the dictation key (Fn by default) and release it when you finish.

Speak normally

Speech is recognized on your device, with no connection needed.

Text appears in any app

Messages, mail, notes or a browser: it is inserted where your cursor is. The Mac app also drops hesitation sounds such as um and uh.

Your voice stays on your device

Speech recognition runs locally on your iPhone and your Mac. Audio is never sent to a server.

Because nothing is processed in the cloud, dictation also works offline. Privacy policy

Questions

Does it work offline?

Yes. Recognition runs on the device, so no internet connection is needed to dictate.

Which languages are supported?

Speakboard uses the Whisper speech model, which supports about 99 languages.

Is one subscription enough for iPhone and Mac?

Yes. Pro is tied to one account and works on both devices.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel whenever you want and Pro stays active until the end of the paid period.

Is my voice uploaded?

No. Audio is processed on your device and is not sent anywhere.
