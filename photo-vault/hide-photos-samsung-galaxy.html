How to hide photos on a Samsung Galaxy (Secure Folder, Gallery, and an app)

You can hide photos on a Galaxy without installing anything. This is what Gallery and Secure Folder do, what Samsung says about their limits, and when an app makes sense.

Maruta G · 25 November 2026 · Leer en español

We make Photo Vault, an Android and iPhone vault app, and this guide starts with what Samsung already gives you. For many people Secure Folder is enough, and we say where it is not. Steps and limits come from the Samsung and Google pages linked below, checked on 25 November 2026. Menu names can differ by country, device and One UI version.

Short answer: to keep photos away from casual browsing, move them to Secure Folder. Hiding an album in Gallery is quicker but Samsung's page describes only the steps and does not mention a lock. An app helps when you do not want a Samsung account in the picture or want a lock that is separate from the phone's screen lock, but our app handles photos only.

Step by step

  1. Hide an album in Gallery (quick, no lock mentioned). Open Gallery, tap Albums, tap View all, open the three-dot menu, tap Hide albums and switch on the albums you want to hide. This is Samsung's page for Australian devices; it does not say whether hidden albums are protected by a lock and points to Secure Folder if you want files removed from Gallery completely.
  2. Set up Secure Folder. Go to Settings, Security and privacy, More security settings, Secure Folder. Sign in or create a Samsung account, wait for setup, tap Done and choose a lock type (PIN, pattern or password). Samsung says it encrypts all data stored there.
  3. Move photos from Gallery. In Gallery, select the photos, tap More options and choose Move to Secure Folder. Choosing copy creates another version of the file inside Secure Folder; choosing move transfers the original. Samsung says this only works with Samsung apps such as My Files, Gallery and Voice Recorder.
  4. Open them later. Open the Secure Folder app and tap Gallery for photos. To hide the Secure Folder icon, go to Settings, Security and privacy, More security settings, Secure Folder and turn off Add Secure Folder to Apps screen; it stays reachable through Settings.
  5. Know the limits before you rely on it. Secure Folder needs a Samsung account to set up and to reset a forgotten PIN. Samsung says data backup is no longer available for Secure Folder, that you move it to a new phone with Smart Switch, and that restoring from an older backup is not available on devices with Android 16 and One UI 8 or higher. Clearing the Secure Folder app's data deletes everything in it.
  6. Or use Locked Folder in Google Photos. In Google Photos tap Collections, then Locked, then Set up Locked Folder. It needs Android 6 or later and uses your screen lock as its password. Google says items you do not back up are stored only on that device and can be lost if you uninstall Photos, clear its data or factory reset.

When an app helps

Secure Folder is a Samsung feature that needs a Samsung account, and Locked Folder follows your screen lock and the Google Photos app. An app helps when you want a lock of its own that does not depend on either, on any Android phone, or when you move between Samsung and other brands.

That is what Photo Vault on Google Play does, within limits. You import photos with the Android Photo Picker, so it does not ask for access to your whole gallery. They are encrypted on the phone with the Tink library and a key in the Android Keystore, behind a PIN of 4 to 8 digits or the fingerprint or face unlock already set on the phone. There is no account, no cloud, no ads and no analytics, and its Data safety label declares no data collected.

What it does not do: it handles photos only, not video, which Secure Folder does handle. It has no decoy PIN, disguised icon or album passwords. There is no backup of any kind: if you uninstall it, clear its data or change phones, the vault is permanently deleted and a forgotten PIN cannot be recovered, so Secure Folder and Smart Switch are better if moving phones matters. Questions people ask

How do I hide photos on a Samsung Galaxy?

Move them to Secure Folder: select the photos in Gallery, tap More options and choose Move to Secure Folder. You can also hide an album in Gallery from Albums, View all, the three-dot menu and Hide albums.

Is Samsung Secure Folder safe?

Samsung says it encrypts all data stored in Secure Folder and that you set a lock for it. Two limits: you need a Samsung account, and Samsung says data backup is no longer available for Secure Folder, so move it to a new phone with Smart Switch.

Can I hide photos in Samsung Gallery without Secure Folder?

Yes, you can hide an album from Albums, View all, the three-dot menu and Hide albums. Samsung's page for that does not mention a lock, so treat it as hiding, not protection.

What happens to Secure Folder when I get a new Galaxy?

Samsung says to use Smart Switch to transfer Secure Folder data, and that restoring from a previous backup is not available on devices with Android 16 and One UI 8 or higher. Move it while the old phone still works.

Do I need an app if I have a Samsung phone?

Not necessarily. An app helps if you want a lock that is separate from your Samsung account and screen lock. Photo Vault for Android keeps photos only, not video, and has no backup.
