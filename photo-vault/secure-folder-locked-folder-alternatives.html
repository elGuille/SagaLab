Samsung Secure Folder and Google Photos Locked Folder alternatives (2026)

Both built-in folders keep photos off your gallery, but each has a backup or portability catch. This is what Samsung and Google say, and what to use instead.

Maruta G · 25 November 2026

We make Photo Vault, an Android and iPhone vault app, so the alternatives section includes our own app. What the two folders do comes from Samsung and Google support pages checked on 25 November 2026; menus and availability vary by phone and software version.

Short answer: Secure Folder is the better choice on a Galaxy phone if you move phones with Smart Switch while the old phone still works. Locked Folder works on more Android phones but leaves unbacked-up items on the device only. Neither is a backup, so keep a copy of anything you cannot lose.

What Samsung Secure Folder does

Per Samsung's support page, Secure Folder stores photos, videos, files, apps and data, encrypted by Samsung Knox. You sign in with a Samsung account and choose a lock, then use Move to Secure Folder from Gallery. The icon can be hidden from the Apps screen. It is a Samsung feature, so it does not exist on other brands.

The catches are in Samsung's backup page: data backup is no longer available for Secure Folder, you use Smart Switch to move it to a new phone, and restoring from a previous backup is not available on devices with Android 16 and One UI 8 or higher. The page does not describe any way to recover the contents if you lose the phone before moving it.

What Google Photos Locked Folder does

Per Google's help page, Locked Folder keeps items out of the Photos grid, memories, search and albums, and out of other apps with photo access. Backup for it is optional. With backup off, items live only on that device, and a new phone does not receive them through USB or cloud transfers. Copies, edits and shared versions of a photo are not moved and stay where they are.

Alternatives

  • Files by Google Safe folder. Works on Android 8.0 and later with a PIN or pattern, for any Android phone. Google says forgetting the PIN or pattern locks you out, that Back up to Google Drive is not available inside it, and that anyone who gains unauthorized access to the device's internal storage may be able to reach the files. Installed apps cannot be moved into it.
  • Photo Vault on Google Play (ours). Works on any Android phone, with no Samsung or Google account, a PIN of 4 to 8 digits or the phone's fingerprint or face unlock, photos encrypted on the device with Tink and an Android Keystore key, no ads and no analytics. It is worse than both built-in folders on three points: photos only, no video, no backup of any kind, and no way to move the vault to a new phone, because the listing says uninstalling, clearing data or changing phones permanently deletes it. It also has no decoy PIN or disguised icon on Android.
  • Other vault apps. Some list a cloud copy, which solves the new-phone problem at the cost of your photos leaving the device. Compared in the best apps to hide photos on Android.

Questions people ask

Does Samsung Secure Folder back up my photos?

No. Samsung says data backup is no longer available for Secure Folder. To move it to a new phone, Samsung says to use Smart Switch.

Is Secure Folder only on Samsung phones?

Yes, it is a Samsung feature for Galaxy phones and tablets, and Samsung notes some older models no longer support it. Google Photos Locked Folder works on Android 6 or later.

What happens to Locked Folder photos if I change phones?

If you did not back up Locked Folder, Google says the items are not transferred in phone-to-phone or cloud transfers and are stored only on the old device. With backup on, you can access them from other devices signed in to your Google Account.

What is a good alternative to Secure Folder on a non-Samsung phone?

Google Photos Locked Folder, the Files by Google Safe folder (Android 8.0 or later) or a vault app. Each has its own trade-off between backup and keeping photos on the device.

Does Photo Vault for Android back up my photos?

No. Its Google Play listing says there is no cloud backup and no account, and that uninstalling the app, clearing its data or changing phones permanently deletes the vault. Export any photo you want to keep first.
