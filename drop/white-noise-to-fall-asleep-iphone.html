How to use white noise to fall asleep on iPhone (Drop guide)

A steady sound covers the noises that wake you: traffic, a neighbour, a dripping tap. This is how to set it up on an iPhone so it plays all night or stops on its own.

Maruta G · 11 October 2026, updated 24 October 2026 · Leer en español

We make Drop, the app this guide uses. Every step also works with other sound apps, and the first option below is free and already on your iPhone.

White noise, rain or a fan do not make you sleep; what they do is mask sudden sounds, so a car door or a voice in the corridor stands out less. Whether it helps you is personal, and so is the sound: some people prefer rain or a fan to pure noise. If you have ongoing trouble sleeping, talk to a doctor.

No app needed: iOS has Background Sounds (Settings, Accessibility, Audio & Visual) with a few sounds such as noise, rain, ocean and stream, and its own timer to stop them. If that is enough for you, use it.

Step by step

  1. Install Drop. Get Drop from the App Store. Ten sounds are free, with no ads and no account: rain, dewdrops, fire, forest, river, coffee shop, ocean waves, thunderstorm, bus and fan.
  2. Pick one steady sound. Choose a sound without sudden changes. A fan, rain or the bus are steadier than birds or a storm. Try one for a few nights before switching.
  3. Set the volume low. Turn it down until it just covers the room's noises. It should not be the loudest thing you hear, and lower is gentler on your ears over a whole night.
  4. Lock the screen. Drop keeps playing with the screen locked, so the phone can stay dark on the nightstand.
  5. Make it stop on its own, if you want. Tap Timer on the sound screen and pick 15, 30 or 45 minutes, 1, 2 or 8 hours. The sound fades out over the last 30 seconds and stops. Or choose no timer and leave it on all night. You can also use the Clock app (Timers, When Timer Ends, Stop Playing).
  6. Take a few slow breaths first. Before you start the sound, open Drop's breathing guide, set how long each breath lasts (2 to 8 seconds) and how many cycles, and follow the animation. Each session grows the plant in the app, which makes it easier to keep the habit.

What Drop does and does not do

Drop has 53 nature, ambient and noise sounds, nine of them free, a breathing guide, a plant that grows with each session, streaks, widgets and no ads. It works offline, needs no account, and runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Drop Pro unlocks the other 44 sounds, by subscription or with a one-time lifetime purchase.

It has a sleep timer (15 minutes to 8 hours, with a 30-second fade-out), plays one sound at a time with no mixer, and since version 2.10 has white noise free and pink, brown and deep brown noise with Drop Pro, as looped tracks with no equaliser; since version 2.11 (24 October 2026) the loops have no gap at the join and the sound comes back after a phone call. More on Drop's page. If you want to mix sounds or fine-tune noise, other apps do it better, and we say which in the best white noise and sleep sound apps for iPhone.

Questions people ask

Does white noise help you fall asleep?

It masks sudden noises such as traffic or voices, which some people find helps them fall and stay asleep. It does not work for everyone; try it for a few nights.

How do I make white noise stop automatically on iPhone?

In Drop, tap Timer on the sound screen and pick 15, 30 or 45 minutes, 1, 2 or 8 hours; the sound fades out and stops. Without an app, use the Clock app: set a timer and choose When Timer Ends, Stop Playing.

Can I play white noise on iPhone without an app?

Yes. Settings, Accessibility, Audio & Visual, Background Sounds plays a few built-in sounds such as noise, rain, ocean and stream, and can stop on its own with a timer.

Does Drop keep playing when the screen is locked?

Yes. The sound keeps playing with the screen locked.

Is Drop free?

It is free to download, with nine sounds free and no ads. Drop Pro unlocks the other 44 sounds by subscription or with a one-time lifetime purchase.
