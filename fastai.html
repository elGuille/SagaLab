Ask AI

AI models from several companies, such as Gemini, Claude, Grok, DeepSeek and Perplexity Sonar, in one chat on your iPhone. Pick one, switch whenever you like.

A few free messages a day. Pro: $49.99 a year on the US App Store, with a 3-day free trial.

Download on the App Store

On the App Store it is called “All AI Models in One: Ask AI”. It used to be called Fast AI, and its description still uses that name.

What it does

  • Models from several companies, one appSuch as Gemini, Claude, Grok, DeepSeek and Perplexity Sonar. One subscription instead of one per company.
  • Switch in the middle of a chatAsk the same question to another model, or move to a faster one for quick tasks and a stronger one for hard ones.
  • Write, rewrite, summarize and translateOr study a topic and get unstuck on homework.
  • Web search (Pro)It searches the web by itself when your question needs current information.
  • Read aloudHave any answer spoken.
  • In your languageThe interface is in English, Spanish, Portuguese, Japanese and Korean. Chats get automatic titles, so they are easy to find later.

What it does not do

Ask AI is chat only: no images, videos or files. The free messages are a few a day; Pro has a much higher limit, but it is still a limit. It needs iOS 18.6 or later and has only a handful of ratings so far. Its App Store privacy label lists usage data used to track you, and purchases and a user ID linked to you. If you want chat and images in one app, see AI Assistant; for AI video, Dora.

Questions

Which AI models does Ask AI use?

Models from several companies, such as Gemini, Claude, Grok, DeepSeek and Perplexity Sonar. The list is updated as new models come out, and the app shows the current names.

Is Ask AI free?

You can send a few messages every day for free. Pro, a yearly auto-renewing subscription, unlocks every model, web search and a much higher limit. It costs $49.99 a year on the US App Store, with a 3-day free trial; prices are shown in the app before you buy.

Can I switch models in the middle of a chat?

Yes. Change the model at any time and keep the same conversation.

Does it know about current events?

With Pro, it searches the web by itself when your question needs fresh information.

Does it make images or read files?

No. It is chat only: no images, videos or files. For chat plus images, our other app AI Assistant does both.

Who makes it?

Saga Labs, an independent studio. It is not affiliated with the companies whose models it uses; their names belong to their owners.
