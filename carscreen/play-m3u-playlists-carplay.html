How to play your own M3U playlists on CarPlay

Add a playlist you have the right to play, check that your car supports video, and send it to the car screen while parked.

Maruta G · 25 November 2026

We make CarScreen, the app used in this guide. Short answer: put the link of an M3U or M3U8 playlist you have the right to play into CarScreen on your iPhone, check that your car supports video, then connect to CarPlay while parked and send the video to the car display with AirPlay. It works only in cars that support video on the car screen, and only when you are not driving.

What an M3U playlist is, and whose it should be

An M3U or M3U8 playlist is a text file that lists the addresses of media items, often grouped, with names and logos. The HTTP Live Streaming specification (RFC 8216) describes playlists as UTF-8 text files containing URIs and descriptive tags, identified by a path ending in .m3u8 or .m3u or by their content type. The file only points to media; it does not come with the right to play it. CarScreen's own listing says it is only a player: it supplies no channels or content, does not host, include or recommend any, and you are accountable for holding the rights to everything you play. Good sources are a list you wrote for your own video files, your own Jellyfin or Emby server, or a list from a service that is entitled to provide it to you.

Step by step

  1. Check your car first. Video on the car screen works only when you are not driving and only in vehicles that support it. Apple Support says that if your vehicle indicates video playback is not available, the video switches to audio only. CarScreen plays a 5-second test clip before you pay and tells you whether video works in your car.
  2. Use only a playlist you have the right to play. That means a list you made yourself from your own files or media server, or one issued to you by a service that has the right to offer it. Having the link does not give you the right to play what it points to.
  3. Install CarScreen and add the playlist. CarScreen needs iOS 26.0 or later. Paste the playlist link, or scan a QR code if your list has one. The app reads the list and puts each entry under the group it belongs to.
  4. Choose what to show. You can keep several playlists, rename, update or delete each one, show only the groups you pick and save favorites.
  5. Park, connect the iPhone to CarPlay and send the video to the car. Start an entry in CarScreen, tap the AirPlay button and select your CarPlay display, as in Apple's steps. CarScreen's car mode is a landscape screen with large buttons and a quick way to jump to the next entry.
  6. Stop when you drive. Video is for a parked car. Never watch video while driving.

What does not work

  • Streaming services protected by DRM. CarScreen's listing says Netflix, Disney+ and other DRM-protected services do not play on the car screen.
  • Cars without video support. CarScreen is not a CarPlay app; its listing says it uses the AirPlay connection your car offers. If your car does not offer it, the test clip tells you before you pay.
  • Older iPhones. CarScreen's App Store page lists iOS 26.0 as the minimum.
  • Driving. Video is for when the car is parked.

Questions people ask

What is an M3U playlist?

A text file that lists media addresses, usually with group names and logos. The HTTP Live Streaming specification describes such playlists as UTF-8 text files containing URIs and descriptive tags.

Can I play Netflix or Disney+ on my car screen with CarScreen?

No. CarScreen's listing says streaming services protected by DRM do not play on the car screen. It plays your own videos, M3U, M3U8 and HLS lists, and your Jellyfin or Emby server.

Why does my car not show the video?

Video on the car screen needs a car whose maker supports it, and Apple says that if the vehicle indicates video playback is not available, the video switches to audio only. CarScreen's test clip checks this for free before you pay.

Can I watch while driving?

No. Video is meant for a parked car, and CarScreen's listing says to never watch video while driving.

Does CarScreen provide channels?

No. It is only a player. You bring your own playlists and servers and are responsible for holding the rights to what you play.

Is CarScreen free?

You can try it in your car free for 3 days, and the car check before you pay is free. After the trial it is paid.
