Dancely: Support

App: Dancely: AI Dance Video (iPhone)
Developer: Saga AI Research Labs
Contact: aisagalab@gmail.com

Write to us at aisagalab@gmail.com for anything about the app. To help us find your case, tell us the date and time, the template you used and what happened.

Subscription and purchases

  • Payments are made through your Apple Account. To cancel or change a subscription: on your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Dancely. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date so you are not charged for the next period.
  • Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.
  • Bought on another iPhone or reinstalled the app? Use Restore Purchases in the app.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Coins pay for videos. If you were charged but the coins or the subscription did not appear, write to us with the date of the purchase.

Deleting your account and your data

  • In the app, open Settings and tap Delete Account & Data.
  • For anything else about your data, write to aisagalab@gmail.com. The Privacy Policy explains what we keep and who receives it.

Problems with a video

  • If a video fails, takes too long or does not look right, write to us with the date and time and the template you used, and we will check it.
  • Photos work best with one person, the whole body visible, good light and a plain background.
  • Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed to it.
  • To report a video or a photo that should not be there, write to us.
