Dancely: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-15
Developer: Saga AI Research Labs
App: Dancely: AI Dance Video (iPhone)

1. Summary

Dancely turns a photo you choose into a dance video. To do that, the photo is sent to third-party AI services. The app asks for your permission before it sends anything to them. Dancely has no advertising, and it does not ask for your name or email.

2. Your photo and the videos

  • When you create a video, the photo you pick (it can show a face and a body) is uploaded to our storage on Google Firebase (Firebase Storage) and sent to Kie (kie.ai), an AI video service, which generates the video with its AI models.
  • The generated videos are kept on our server so that you can see them again in My Videos.
  • We do not use your photos or videos to train AI models, and we do not sell them or share them with anyone else.
  • Only use photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed to it.

3. What else we keep

  • An anonymous Firebase user ID, created when you open the app. It is not linked to your name or email.
  • Your coin balance and the history of your video jobs.
  • Your purchases. Apple processes the payment. To know what you have bought, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives the purchase receipt and the anonymous user ID. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

4. Third-party services

Google Firebase (Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy), Kie (kie.ai) and RevenueCat. They receive only what is described above.

5. Age

Dancely is for people aged 17 and over.

6. Deleting your data

In Settings, Delete Account & Data deletes your Firebase account, your coin balance and your video jobs. To have your uploaded photos and generated videos deleted as well, or for any other request about your data, write to aisagalab@gmail.com.

7. Changes

We will update this page if this changes. The effective date shows the latest version.

Contact and support: aisagalab@gmail.com
