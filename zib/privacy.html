Zib privacy: what stays on your iPhone

Zib runs Lia, its AI friend, on your iPhone: her language model, her voice and her memory. What you tell her stays on the device, and you can see and delete what she remembers.

Updated 12 November 2026. This page covers the Zib app; the general Saga Labs privacy policy covers our website and other apps.

Your conversations

  • Lia's language model, her voice and her memory run on your iPhone. What you say or type and what she answers are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Your voice is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition on the device. Zib does not record or keep audio.
  • Lia's memory (the conversation, the facts she keeps about you and a short summary) is stored only on your iPhone.
  • There is no account and no sign-up.

Seeing and deleting what Lia remembers

  • In Settings, What Lia remembers shows every fact she keeps and the summary of your life.
  • Swipe a fact to make her forget it, or tap Forget everything to delete her whole memory.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything Zib stored on the device.

What the app sends over the network

  • Downloading Lia's models. The first time, Zib downloads the AI models (about 1.3 GB) from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). Hugging Face sees a normal download request from your IP address; it never sees your conversations. After that, talking to Lia does not need the internet.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions are paid through Apple. Zib asks Apple whether you have Zib Plus or Zib Lite; we never see your payment details.

What stays on the device

  • Your settings, the voice you chose, and a few counters (for example, how many voice minutes you used today and which days you talked).
  • Lia's messages to you are local notifications written on the iPhone. You can turn them off in Settings.
  • A small technical log (timings and errors, without what you said) used to fix problems. It is not sent anywhere.

No ads, no tracking

Zib has no advertising and no analytics or tracking SDK.

Children

Zib is meant for people aged 13 and over.
