Zib: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-14
Developer: Saga AI Research Labs
App: Zib (iPhone)

1. Summary

Your conversations with Lia never leave your iPhone. The language model, speech recognition, the voice and Lia's memory run on your device. Saga AI Research Labs does not receive your conversations, your voice or what Lia remembers. The app has no account, no advertising and no analytics or crash-reporting services.

2. What stays on your iPhone

  • What you say and type, and Lia's answers. Speech is transcribed on the device with Apple's on-device speech recognition, and Lia's voice is generated on the device.
  • Lia's memory: the things you tell her, saved in a local file in the app's storage. It is not synced to iCloud. You can see it and delete one item or all of it in Settings.
  • Lia's journal and your app settings.

3. Model download

The first time you use Zib, it downloads the AI models (about 1.3 GB to start) from Hugging Face, a public model host. This download does not include anything you say. After that, Zib works without an internet connection.

4. Purchases

Zib Plus and Zib Lite are subscriptions sold through Apple. Apple processes the payment. To know what you have bought, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives the purchase receipt and an anonymous app user ID. It does not receive your name, email, conversations or voice. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, Lia can send you up to 2 messages a day about something you talked about. These are local notifications created on your iPhone, not sent from a server. You can turn them off at any time.

6. Microphone

The microphone is used only while you are in a voice call with Lia, and the audio is processed on the device.

7. Children

Zib is not directed at children under 13.

8. Deleting your data

Delete Lia's memory in Settings, or delete the app to remove everything stored by it. We hold no copy.

9. Changes

We will update this page if this changes. The effective date shows the latest version.
