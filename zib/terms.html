Zib terms of use

The short version: Lia is an AI character to talk with, not a professional; you need to be 13 or older; subscriptions are paid and cancelled through Apple.

Updated 12 November 2026.

What Zib is

  • Zib is an app to talk and write with Lia, an AI character. Lia is not a person, a therapist, a doctor or a lawyer, and what she says is not professional advice.
  • Lia can be wrong or forget things. Don't rely on her for anything important.
  • If you are in crisis or in danger, contact your local emergency number. Lia will tell you the same.

Who can use it

You must be at least 13 years old, or the minimum age in your country if it is higher.

How you use it

  • Don't use Zib to break the law or to harm yourself or others.
  • Don't try to make Lia produce sexual, hateful or dangerous content. She is built to refuse it.

Plans and payments

  • Free: unlimited text chat, memory included, and 10 minutes of voice calls a day.
  • Zib Plus (monthly or yearly) gives voice calls with no daily limit. Zib Lite (monthly) gives 60 minutes of voice a day. The prices are the ones shown in the app before you buy.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID. Subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period. You can manage or cancel them in your iPhone's Settings. Refunds are handled by Apple.
  • If a free trial is offered and you don't cancel before it ends, the subscription starts and is charged.

Your data

Your conversations and Lia's memory stay on your iPhone and you can delete them at any time. See the Zib privacy page.

Changes and the rest

We may update Zib and these terms; the date above shows the latest version. Everything not covered here follows the general Saga Labs terms and Apple's standard licence agreement (EULA) for App Store apps.
