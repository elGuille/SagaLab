Best apps with all AI models in one place for iPhone (2026)

Seven iPhone apps that put models from several AI companies in one place, compared on what their App Store pages name, what they cost, and what you would pay for each maker's own app instead.

Maruta G · 26 November 2026 · 日本語

Yes, several iPhone apps put models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI and others in one app, so you pick a model for each question instead of paying for each maker's app. On their US App Store pages on 26 November 2026, Chat AI: Ask Agent Anything names models from all six makers we looked for, Sider names the longest list, and Poe has the cheapest monthly plan ($4.99) and lets you compare models in one chat. Buying ChatGPT Plus, Claude Pro, Google AI Pro and SuperGrok separately comes to $89.98 a month.

Disclosure: we make Ask AI, number 4 on this list. Everything about the other apps comes from their own App Store pages in the US store and from Apple's public iTunes lookup, checked on 26 November 2026. We only count the models each page names: an app can offer more inside, and the names on a page can lag behind what the app uses today. None of these seven apps is made by OpenAI, Anthropic, Google or xAI.

The ranking

  1. Chat AI: Ask Agent Anything: the most model makers named, with images, documents and voice.
  2. Sider: the longest list of models, including Kimi, GLM and Qwen, and more than 30 file types.
  3. Poe: compare models in one chat, image and video generators, and the cheapest monthly plan.
  4. Ask AI: five model makers in one plain chat, switch mid-conversation, a few free messages a day.
  5. ChatOn: four model makers, plus images and videos made from your photos.
  6. Genspark: an AI workspace for slides, sheets and docs, with GPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok in its chat.
  7. Perplexity: search with cited sources; its page names OpenAI, Anthropic and Meta.

How we ordered them: first by how many of six model makers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI, DeepSeek and Perplexity) the App Store page names, then by track record and extras where that is close. Poe names only three makers on its page, but it has image and video models, side-by-side comparison and far more ratings than Ask AI, so we put it ahead of ours.

One app or four subscriptions?

Buying the main paid plan of each maker in its own iPhone app costs, on the US App Store: ChatGPT Plus $19.99, Claude Pro $20.00 a month, Google Gemini (Google AI Pro) $19.99 and Grok (SuperGrok) $30.00. That is $89.98 for one month of all four. Among the apps above, Poe Basic is $4.99 a month, Chat AI sells lifetime access from $39.99, and Sider (Pocket), Poe (Basic) and Ask AI have $49.99 yearly plans.

What you give up: each all-in-one app sets its own usage limits on top of the models, and none of these listings gives a number for them (ChatOn's says “unlimited access to all features”). Ask AI's Pro, for example, has a weekly limit. If you spend all day in one model, its maker's own plan may give you more of it.

1. Chat AI: the most model makers named

Chat AI: Ask Agent Anything names GPT-6, Claude Opus 5, Gemini 3.1 Pro, Gemini 3.8 Flash, Grok 4.7, DeepSeek V4 Pro and Perplexity on its App Store page, the only one here that names all six makers. It also makes images, restyles your photos, reads PDF, TXT and DOCX files and talks back hands-free. It charges by the week ($7.39 to $11.99) or once for life ($39.99 or $69.99). Some of its in-app purchases are called “Ask AI Premium”, but it is not our Ask AI. Best if you want every big name and lots of extras in one app.

2. Sider: the longest list of models

Sider lists models by maker: five from OpenAI (GPT-6 Luna, GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6.1 Sol, GPT-5.6 Terra, GPT-5.4 mini), five from Anthropic (Claude Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5 and more), Gemini 3.8 Flash, Grok 4.6, two DeepSeek models, and Kimi K3, GLM-5 and Qwen3.8-Max, which none of the others name. It reads more than 30 file types, makes images, keeps a knowledge base and has a task agent called Sider Hand. Plans run from Basic at $9.99 a month to Unlimited at $39.99, or Pocket at $49.99 a year. Best if you work with files and want the widest choice of models.

3. Poe: compare models side by side

Poe is from Quora. Its listing says you can combine and compare models in a single chat, make images and videos with FLUX, Ideogram, Veo 2, Runway and others, and use or build custom bots. It has free bots, and the cheapest monthly plan here: Basic at $4.99 a month or $49.99 a year (Plus is $19.99 a month). The models its page names (o3, GPT-4.5, Claude 3.7 Sonnet, Gemini 2.0) are older than the ones the other pages name, so check the app for its current list. Best if you want to compare answers and also make images and video.

4. Ask AI: several model makers in one plain chat

Ask AI is ours. Its listing names Gemini, Claude, Grok, DeepSeek and Perplexity Sonar, and you can change the model at any time and keep the same conversation. You get a few free messages a day with the free model; Pro unlocks every model, web search and a much higher limit, at $7.99 a week or $49.99 a year in the US, each with a 3-day free trial. It reads photos you attach and can read answers aloud.

Where others beat it: it is chat only, with no images, videos or file reading (Chat AI, Sider, Poe and ChatOn do those). Pro still has a weekly limit. It has only 3 ratings in the US App Store, against thousands to hundreds of thousands for the others, and its page does not name OpenAI's models, which every other app here mentions. Best if you want to try several model makers in one simple chat, starting free.

5. ChatOn: models plus photos and video

ChatOn says it is built on GPT-5, Gemini 3.5, Claude Sonnet 5 and Sonar. Around the chat it makes images and videos from your photos, summarises PDFs and YouTube links and searches the web. Weekly plans are $6.99 to $7.99, and its page also lists plans at $29.99 and $39.99. Best if you want an all-round assistant that also edits photos.

6. Genspark: a workspace, not just a chat

Genspark is an AI workspace: it builds slides, spreadsheets and documents, takes meeting notes and writes code. Its chat offers GPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok, which its listing calls free, along with image models such as Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image, FLUX and Seedream. Plus is $24.99 a month or $239.99 a year, with credit packs on top. Best if you want finished work, not only answers.

7. Perplexity: search with sources

Perplexity answers with cited sources and says it uses the top models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta and more. The app is free; Pro is listed at $20.00 and $200.00 and Max at $200.00. Its page names companies rather than models, so it has the fewest makers by our count. Best if most of your questions are about finding current, checkable information.

How to choose

  • Every big model maker and lots of extras: Chat AI.
  • The widest model list and files: Sider.
  • Comparing answers, plus images and video: Poe.
  • Several makers in one simple chat, starting free: Ask AI.
  • Chat plus photo and video edits: ChatOn.
  • Slides, sheets and docs: Genspark.
  • Answers with sources: Perplexity.

Is there an app with all AI models in one place?

No app names every model, but Chat AI's App Store page names models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI, DeepSeek and Perplexity, and Sider's names five of those makers plus Kimi, GLM and Qwen. They cost less than one month of the four official plans ($89.98), with usage limits set by each app.

Frequently asked questions

Which app has GPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok in one place?

On their US App Store pages on 26 November 2026, Chat AI: Ask Agent Anything, Sider and Genspark name all four. Chat AI also names DeepSeek and Perplexity. Ask AI, which we make, names Claude, Gemini and Grok, plus DeepSeek and Perplexity Sonar.

Is one app cheaper than paying for ChatGPT Plus, Claude Pro, Google AI Pro and SuperGrok?

On price, yes. Those four plans cost $89.98 a month together on the US App Store, while Poe Basic is $4.99 a month, Chat AI sells lifetime access from $39.99, and Sider Pocket, Poe Basic and Ask AI each have a $49.99 yearly plan. The all-in-one apps set their own usage limits, so check how much of each model a plan gives you.

Can I switch AI models in the same chat?

Yes in some of them. Poe's listing says you can combine and compare models in a single chat, and Ask AI's says you can change the model at any time and keep the same conversation.

Is there a free app with all the AI models?

Genspark's listing offers free chat with GPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok, Poe has free bots, and Ask AI gives a few free messages a day with its free model. Free use is limited in all of them; in Ask AI the other models need Pro.

Are these apps made by OpenAI, Anthropic or Google?

No. All seven are made by other companies that use those models. Ask AI's listing says it is not affiliated with the companies whose models it uses.
