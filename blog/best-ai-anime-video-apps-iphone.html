Best AI anime video apps for iPhone (2026)

Only one of these names anime stories in its listing. The others get there through prompts, or stop at a portrait.

Maruta G · 2 November 2026 · ES · FR

Disclosure: we make Dora, number 3 on this list. Everything about the other apps comes from their own App Store pages. Written from App Store pages on 2 November 2026. We ran no quality test.

On iPhone, anime video comes from two kinds of app: those with templates and a guided flow, and general generators where you write the style into the prompt. A character that stays the same from clip to clip is still the hard part, so keep episodes short and reuse one reference image.

1. Anijam

Its listing names anime-style stories and an AI video agent.

2. Kling AI

Up to 15 seconds in 1080p or 4K, per its listing. Anime is a style you describe.

3. Dora

A general generator: its listing has no anime mode, so you write the style in the prompt. Short clips, not episodes.

4. PixVerse Lite

Text and image to video with templates, per its listing.

5. ToonMe

It turns a selfie into a hand-drawn style portrait, per its listing.

Can I turn my photo into an anime video?

Yes, with a generator that accepts a photo, by describing the style. Results vary, and we ran no test.

Can I use characters from a series?

Characters from existing series are usually protected, and apps may block them. Make your own.
