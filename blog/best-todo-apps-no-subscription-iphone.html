Best to-do apps with no subscription (one-time purchase) for iPhone (2026)

Five iPhone to-do apps where you pay once, or never, and nothing renews. What each one costs, what stays free and what it leaves out.

Maruta G · 25 November 2026 · Español

Things 3, Do Next, 3 Tasks, Daylist and SimplyDone are five iPhone to-do apps with no subscription: Things 3 is a single paid download ($6.99), and the other four are free to start with a one-time purchase for the extras. Pick Things 3 for projects and areas, Do Next or SimplyDone for a plain list, Daylist for lists that restart each day, and 3 Tasks for a hard limit of three tasks a day.

Disclosure: we make 3 Tasks, number 3 on this list. Everything about the other apps comes from their own App Store listings, checked in the US App Store on 25 November 2026, and we say plainly where they beat us. Apple Reminders is free and already on your iPhone, so it needs no purchase; the apps below are for a different way of working.

The ranking

  1. Things 3: best for projects and a full planner, paid once.
  2. Do Next: best for one plain list in four buckets.
  3. 3 Tasks: made for a hard limit of three tasks a day, with a timer on each.
  4. Daylist: best for daily lists that start fresh.
  5. SimplyDone: best for lists with steps, notes and no ads after one purchase.

1. Things 3: a full planner for one price

Things 3 costs $6.99 on iPhone and has no subscription. It organises work into projects, with to-dos inside them, and the listing says sync is provided free through Things Cloud. Mac, iPad and Apple Vision Pro versions are sold separately, so the $6.99 covers the iPhone app only. Best if you want a serious planner and are happy to pay for it once.

2. Do Next: one list, four buckets

Do Next keeps a single list with four categories: Now, Next, Later and Someday. You swipe to complete, edit or delete, and sync runs over iCloud. The listing says there is no monthly subscription and that one in-app purchase unlocks sections editing, configurable checkboxes, colour themes, widgets and custom app icons. Best if you want the least app possible.

3. 3 Tasks: three slots and a stopwatch

3 Tasks is one screen with room for three tasks a day. Each task has a start and pause timer that keeps counting with the screen locked, and Repeat Daily brings back a task you do every day. A home screen widget shows your three. The app is free, with no ads and no account, and the three tasks, the timer, the widget and dark mode stay free. 3 Tasks Pro is a one-time purchase with no subscription: it adds history with the time each task took, a streak, CSV export and four extra colour themes.

Where others beat it: it holds only three tasks, so it is no use as a project planner or a long backlog; Things 3 does that. Do Next has four buckets for the tasks you are not doing today. It uses Firebase Analytics, which is declared on its privacy label.

4. Daylist: a fresh list every day

Daylist gives you a list for today, with reminders on a tap, and starts each day fresh so you do not manage long-term lists. Its listing says no ads, no sign-up and no subscription: you try it for a week and can keep using it forever with a one-time purchase. Best if you want a daily list and nothing else.

5. SimplyDone: lists with steps and notes

SimplyDone is the one here with the most structure: lists with icons and colour-coded groups, steps under a task, notes linked to lists, search, and 30 days in Recently Deleted. It is free with ads, and its Pro upgrade removes them as a one-time purchase, not a subscription, restorable with your Apple Account. Best if you want lists with detail and are happy to pay once to remove ads.

How to choose

  • Projects and a full planner: Things 3.
  • One plain list in four buckets: Do Next.
  • A hard limit of three tasks a day, with a timer: 3 Tasks.
  • A list that restarts every day: Daylist.
  • Detailed lists, ad-free after one purchase: SimplyDone.

Is there a to-do app for iPhone with no subscription?

Yes: all five apps above. Things 3 is a single $6.99 download; Do Next, 3 Tasks, Daylist and SimplyDone are free to start and have a one-time purchase instead of a subscription.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best one-time purchase to-do app for iPhone?

It depends on what you need. Things 3 is the deepest, with projects and areas, at $6.99 on iPhone. Do Next and SimplyDone suit a plain list, and 3 Tasks suits a hard limit of three tasks a day.

Can I use these apps without an account?

Per their listings, 3 Tasks, Daylist and SimplyDone need no account or sign-up. Do Next syncs through iCloud. Things 3 sync runs through its own Things Cloud, which is free.

Does Apple Reminders cost anything?

No. Apple Reminders is built into the iPhone and has no purchase. The apps here are for people who want a different way of working, such as a daily limit or projects.
