Trump family crypto after the midterms: what Democrats plan to investigate, and how WLFI, TRUMP and USD1 moved

What has been reported, with dates and sources, and what the prices did in the week after the vote.

Maruta G · 8 November 2026 · Leer en español

After Democrats won the House in the 3 November 2026 midterms, Rep. Robert Garcia, expected to chair the House Oversight Committee, signalled investigations into the Trump family's crypto businesses, which according to crypto.news could begin as early as February 2027. In the week after the vote, CoinGecko data show the TRUMP meme coin down about 32%, World Liberty Financial's WLFI token down about 8%, and the USD1 stablecoin holding close to $1.

What has been reported

According to crypto.news, which cited NBC News and MS NOW projections, Democrats won control of the House. It reported that Garcia answered the result with two words, “Lawyer up,” and that committees could seek documents and testimony by subpoena.

The targets named in that reporting are:

  • World Liberty Financial, its WLFI token and its USD1 stablecoin, including its foreign ownership. crypto.news reported that an investment vehicle of the UAE's Sheikh Tahnoon bin Zayed took a 49% stake, for a reported $500 million, shortly before the January 2025 inauguration.
  • World Liberty Trust Company, the planned trust bank for USD1. Reuters reported that the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency gave it preliminary conditional approval on 14 August 2026, and that it is not final (Reuters, via The Star).
  • The TRUMP meme coin. According to crypto.news, Trump reported more than $1 billion of crypto-related income for 2025, about $635 million of it in royalties tied to the coin, and Reuters estimated that the family's crypto ventures earned at least $2.3 billion.
  • The pardon of Binance founder Changpeng Zhao. crypto.news reported that Zhao denies business ties to the family and says he never discussed any clemency arrangement.
  • Business dealings of other family members.

White House spokeswoman Lauren Bis told Newsweek, as cited by crypto.news, that the Democrats' plans are a continuation of what she described as a long-running witch hunt against the president. On 8 November the Financial Times carried the story on its front page under the headline Crypto industry braces for reckoning over Trump ties.

What to watch next

  • When the committees start. crypto.news reported that investigations could begin as early as February 2027, once the new House is in place.
  • The CLARITY Act. CNBC reported that the market-structure bill had already failed a Senate cloture vote in September; analysts it quoted expect either a tougher Democratic version next year or no movement until 2028.
  • The trust bank. World Liberty Trust Company's approval is preliminary and conditional; the OCC still has to give final approval (Reuters).

Questions people ask

Are Democrats investigating Trump's crypto?

According to crypto.news, after winning the House on 3 November 2026, Democrats plan to look at World Liberty Financial, USD1, the TRUMP meme coin and related deals, and investigations could begin as early as February 2027.

How did the TRUMP coin react to the midterms?

On CoinGecko it went from $1.71 at the 1 November close to about $1.16 on 8 November, a fall of about 32%, with the largest daily drop on 4 November.

Did USD1 lose its peg?

No. On CoinGecko it stayed close to $1 in the week after the vote, at about $0.998 on 8 November.
