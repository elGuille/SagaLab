Best Siri AI alternatives for iPhone in the EU (2026)

The new Siri is not coming to iPhones in the EU for now. What you lose, what you keep, and what to use instead.

Maruta G · 19 October 2026 · Leer en español

Disclosure: we make Pinku, one of the apps below. We do not rank it first, because it does not do most of what Siri AI does. What we say about each app comes from its App Store page, and about Siri AI from Apple's own support pages, checked on 19 October 2026.

What is and is not available in the EU

Apple's page on Siri AI is explicit: “Siri AI is not available for iOS, iPadOS, or watchOS in the EU.” It is available on macOS 27 and visionOS 27 for people whose Apple Account region is in the EU, with the device and Siri language set to English. It is also English only everywhere for now, and a beta with a waitlist.

That does not mean the EU gets no Apple Intelligence. Apple says the new features across apps, such as advanced photo editing, the reimagined Image Playground and the intelligent features in Safari, are available to users in the EU on iOS 27, on an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or later). On the same page Apple lists Writing Tools as “available for users without Siri AI writing support”, and the only feature it rules out on EU iPhones is Siri AI. What is missing on an EU iPhone is the new Siri: the one that answers questions about almost anything, understands your messages and emails, sees what is on screen and acts across apps.

The options, by what you need

  1. ChatGPT: best general replacement for asking anything.
  2. Google Gemini: best if your mail and calendar are at Google.
  3. Claude: best for writing and long documents.
  4. Vibe by Mistral: best if you want a European company.
  5. Pinku (ours): best if nothing may leave the phone.
  6. Locally AI: free offline chat, also private.

ChatGPT

The most rated AI app on the App Store, and the most direct replacement for “ask Siri anything”. Free to start, with Go, Plus and Pro plans in the app. It is a cloud service from a US company: its privacy label lists contact info, usage data and user content as linked to you.

Google Gemini

Free, from iOS 17.4, with paid tiers bundled with Google storage. If your mail, calendar and photos are at Google, it is the closest thing to the personal context Siri AI promises, inside Google's apps. Cloud only.

Claude

From Anthropic. Free to start, Pro at €22 a month. The best of these for writing, editing and working through long documents. Cloud only.

Vibe by Mistral

Formerly Le Chat, made by Mistral AI in Paris, the only EU company here. Its privacy label separates contact info, which is linked to you, from usage data and user content, which it declares as not linked. Free, Vibe Pro at €17.99, iOS 16.4 or later.

Pinku (ours)

Pinku is not a Siri replacement: it cannot read your messages, see your screen or act in other apps. What it does is answer on the iPhone itself, with an open model you download once, so the conversation never goes to any server, in the EU or anywhere else, and it works in airplane mode. No account. Its interface is in Spanish, German, French and other languages, and it runs on any iPhone 12 or later with iOS 18.6, with or without Apple Intelligence. The default model is free; Pinku Pro, an in-app purchase, adds larger models, custom instructions, dictation and read aloud. A phone-sized model is much weaker than the cloud apps above.

Locally AI by LM Studio

The free alternative to Pinku for private, offline chat: no login, Data Not Collected, iOS 18.1 or later, and it can also look at images.

Have a Mac?

On a Mac with macOS 27 Golden Gate, Siri AI is available in the EU if your device and Siri language are both English. Apple Intelligence itself needs a Mac with M1 or later.

Questions people ask

Is Siri AI available in the EU?

Not on iPhone. Apple says Siri AI is not available for iOS, iPadOS or watchOS in the EU. It is available on macOS 27 and visionOS 27 for EU accounts with the device and Siri language set to English.

Is Apple Intelligence available in the EU?

Yes. Apple says the Apple Intelligence features across apps, such as advanced photo editing, Image Playground and Safari's intelligent features, are available to users in the EU on iOS 27, on a supported iPhone (iPhone 15 Pro or later). Only Siri AI is missing on iPhone.

What is the best Siri AI alternative on iPhone in Europe?

For asking anything, ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude; Vibe by Mistral if you prefer a European company. None of them reads your messages and acts across apps the way Siri AI does. For a private assistant that works offline, Pinku or Locally AI.

Does Siri AI work in Spanish?

Not yet. Apple says Siri AI is currently available in English.

Is there an AI assistant that keeps my chats on my iPhone?

Yes. On-device apps such as Pinku and Locally AI run the model on the iPhone, so the conversation is not sent to a server. They are less capable than cloud assistants.
