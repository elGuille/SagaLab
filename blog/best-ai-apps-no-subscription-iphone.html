Best AI apps without a subscription (one-time purchase) for iPhone (2026)

For an AI app with no subscription, Locally AI costs nothing and Private LLM is a one-time $4.99. Pinku is ours, third, and needs a $12.99 Pro purchase to chat.

Maruta G · 24 November 2026

Written from App Store pages on 24 November 2026, including each page's list of in-app purchases. We ran no quality test.

Most AI apps charge a weekly, monthly or yearly plan. The apps below run the model on your phone, so there is no server bill for each answer, and that is why some of them can be bought once or cost nothing. The cost is that on-device models are weaker than the big cloud ones. Apps that connect you to cloud models, such as Enclave Pro and Privacy AI, keep subscriptions for that part, and are not ranked here.

Prices and ratings checked on the US App Store on 24 November 2026. Check the in-app purchase list on each page before you buy: it shows the plans the developer sells.

1. Locally AI by LM Studio

Its page lists no in-app purchases. It names Llama 3.2, Gemma, Qwen 3 and DeepSeek on Apple MLX, with no login. If you only want to try local AI, start here.

2. Private LLM

Its page calls it a one-time purchase that does not need a subscription, and says that includes Family Sharing. At $4.99 it is the cheapest paid app on this list, and it names the longest list of model families. Its US rating is 4.1.

3. Pinku

Pinku is a paid app. To chat you need Pinku Pro, and the one-time purchase is $12.99 on its App Store page; Pro is also sold as a yearly plan, so choose the one-time option if you want no renewals. Pro unlocks every model, custom instructions, voice dictation transcribed on the device and photos for Gemma 4 E2B. The listing names 12 open models, runs them on Apple MLX, and says there is no account and no server.

Where others win: Locally AI and Noema cost nothing and Private LLM costs less than half. Pinku's models are weaker than cloud ones and it has no web browsing.

Pinku is in the App Store: Pinku on the App Store. More on its page: aisagalab.com/pinku.

4. Noema

No in-app purchases are listed. It runs GGUF, MLX and other formats, and imports PDFs and EPUBs. Remote providers are optional and you turn them on yourself; whatever those providers charge is between you and them.

5. On-Device AI: Private Workspace

A chat, listening, transcription and notes app that also works on Mac. Its page lists a subscription at $3.99 and $34.99 and lifetime options at $49.99 and $99.99; it does not say what separates the two lifetime tiers.

6. Secret AI

Its description says basic offline features are free and that some premium ones, such as GPU acceleration and unlimited chat sessions, need a one-time purchase or subscription. The page lists lifetime Plus at $59.99 and lifetime Pro at $119.99, the most expensive one-time prices here.

How to choose

  • You want to spend nothing: Locally AI or Noema.
  • You want a small one-time price: Private LLM at $4.99.
  • You want Pinku's model list and accept $12.99 once: Pinku Pro.
  • You want one app for chat, transcription and notes and will pay for lifetime: On-Device AI.

Disclosure: we make Pinku, number 3 on this list. Everything about the other apps comes from their own App Store pages.

Which AI apps for iPhone have no subscription?

On their App Store pages, Locally AI and Noema list no in-app purchases, Private LLM is a one-time $4.99 purchase, and Pinku, On-Device AI and Secret AI each list a lifetime or one-time option.

Why can a local AI app be bought once?

The model runs on your phone, so the developer pays no server cost for each answer. Apps that give you cloud models usually charge a subscription for them.

Is the one-time purchase the whole price?

Check the in-app purchase list on the App Store page. Several of these apps also sell subscriptions or other tiers next to the lifetime one.

Are these as good as ChatGPT?

No. On-device models of a few gigabytes are clearly weaker than frontier cloud models. In return nothing you type needs to leave the phone.
