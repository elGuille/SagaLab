Best Bible stories apps for adults: animated, in episodes (2026)

Five apps that tell Bible stories as short videos, with the phone each one runs on. Ours is on Android only.

Maruta G · 25 November 2026 · Español

Short answer: on iPhone, start with Bible Shorts: Animated Stories or BibleWatch; on Android, Bible Videos is one option, and it is ours. Bible Shorts tells each story as an animated one-minute short, in Bible order, with the first 25 free. BibleWatch tells stories as narrated, scored episodes that its listing calls animated. BibleProject is free and explains each book of the Bible instead of retelling stories. Bible Videos, number 3, is on Google Play only: we found no iPhone version in the App Store, and its listing describes photoreal scenes rather than animation. Disclosure: we make it; see the last section. Facts about the iPhone apps are from their US App Store listings and about Bible Videos from its Google Play listing, all checked on 25 November 2026.

We left out apps whose listings address children, such as Superbook Kids Bible, and live-action series such as The Chosen, a drama about the life of Jesus that its listing says has no subscriptions or paywalls. They are good for different questions.

We ranked on how closely each app matches "Bible stories as short episodes", then on how much is free. We watched none of them end to end; each entry describes what its listing says. We checked platforms on the App Store (iPhone) and on Google Play (Android) on 25 November 2026, by searching each store for the app's name.

1. Bible Shorts: Animated Stories: the closest match to the question

Each story is a short animated film of about a minute with narration and captions, so it works with the sound off. The library follows Bible order and remembers where you stopped, a streak counts daily viewing, and after each story the app asks one question and saves your written answer to a private journal. Tapping Read opens the passage the story comes from; its listing says the scripture text is from the World English Bible. The first 25 stories are free and Pro unlocks the library, at prices its listing gives as $2.99 a week with a 3-day trial, $6.99 a month or $39.99 a year.

2. BibleWatch: narrated, scored episodes

Its listing describes cinematic short episodes of Bible stories, told from Scripture, "narrated, scored, and animated", with a library that keeps growing, background listening and daily quests and streaks. It does not describe prices in the text we read, so check the app before counting on free use.

3. Bible Videos: photoreal short films, Android only

Our app, described in the last section. Placed third because it is Android only, its listing describes a library that is just starting, and its scenes are described as photoreal, which does not match the "animated" in this page's title.

4. Bible Reels: a guided path with quizzes

Short videos on a structured path from Genesis to Revelation, with quizzes and a review of the questions you miss. Its listing calls the videos cinematic and does not say whether they are animated. It suits you if you want to be tested on what you watched.

5. BibleProject: explanations, not retold stories

A nonprofit's free app with short visual videos that explain how each book is structured and what its themes are, plus podcasts and a class on Genesis. Its listing says all of it is free with no paid subscription. It is last only because it explains rather than retells; for understanding a book it may be the best of these.

How to choose

  • iPhone, animated one-minute stories in order: Bible Shorts: Animated Stories.
  • iPhone or Android, narrated and scored episodes: BibleWatch.
  • Android, photoreal short films with the Bible text offline: Bible Videos.
  • Quizzes and a guided path, on iPhone or Android: Bible Reels.
  • Understanding each book, completely free, on iPhone or Android: BibleProject.

Questions people ask

Is there an app that tells Bible stories as animated episodes?

Yes. On iPhone, Bible Shorts: Animated Stories tells stories as animated one-minute films with the first 25 free, and BibleWatch's listing describes narrated, scored and animated episodes.

Is Bible Videos available on iPhone?

Not at the time of writing. Bible Videos is on Google Play for Android; we found no Bible Videos app in the App Store on 25 November 2026.

Which of these apps is completely free?

BibleProject's listing says all of its videos, podcasts and classes are free with no paid subscription. The others have free parts and paid plans.
