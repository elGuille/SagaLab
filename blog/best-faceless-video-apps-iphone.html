Best faceless video apps for iPhone (2026)

For a faceless channel that wants a finished video from a topic, Faceless Video: AI Shorts and AutoAIReel are the two that describe the whole chain in their listings. If you want one clip to build on yourself, Dora is the fit.

A faceless video shows no one on camera: stock or generated scenes, a voiceover, captions and music. On iPhone there are two kinds of app. Some write the script, voice it and cut the scenes for you from a topic. Others generate clips and leave the script, voice and edit to you.

1. Faceless Video: AI Shorts

You pick a topic, a style and a voice, and its listing says it writes the script and adds voiceover, images and music. It names one-tap export to TikTok, YouTube and Instagram and more than ten visual styles.

2. Faceless Reels - AutoAIReel

You enter a script and choose a style. Its listing names AI voiceover, subtitles, background music, avatar narration and lip-sync, and says new users get credits on sign-up to try it.

3. Faceless - AI Video Generator

Text becomes a short reel, with visuals, music, transitions and captions picked automatically, per its listing. You can adjust filters and captions afterwards.

4. Dora

Dora is not a script-to-video tool. You describe a scene or add a photo and it returns a short vertical 9:16 clip, up to 1080p and without a watermark. Its listing says it does not edit long videos on a timeline and does not post to your accounts, so the voiceover and the edit are yours. Our step-by-step for faceless Reels with Dora shows the full flow.

5. VideoGPT

You write a story prompt and pick a style such as cinematic, anime or Pixar, per its listing. It is the most rated app here, so its score rests on far more reviews than the others.

6. Faceless Video - Magic Shorts

Its listing is built around choosing a niche and a posting schedule so the channel keeps publishing. It says Premium starts at $19.99 a month.

How to choose

  • You have a topic and no script: apps 1 to 3 start from there.
  • You have a script: AutoAIReel takes it as written.
  • You want scenes that look like your own idea, or a photo brought to life: Dora or VideoGPT.
  • You want posting handled for you: Magic Shorts names scheduled posting, and Faceless Video: AI Shorts names one-tap export.

What is a faceless video?

A video with no one on camera. It is built from generated or stock scenes with a voiceover, captions and music.

Do I need to record my own voice?

Not with Faceless Video: AI Shorts or AutoAIReel, whose listings name AI voiceover. With Dora you add the voice yourself.

Which app makes a clip from a photo?

Dora takes a photo and returns a short clip. The faceless generators above start from text.

Can I post straight from the app?

Faceless Video: AI Shorts names one-tap export to TikTok, YouTube and Instagram, and Magic Shorts names scheduled posting. Dora saves to your camera roll or shares from the app.
