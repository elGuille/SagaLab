Best Siri AI alternatives for iPhone (2026)

Apple's new Siri is English only, waitlisted, and missing on iPhone in the EU and China. What it does, and what to use where you cannot get it.

Maruta G · 19 October 2026 · Русский · 简体中文

What we say about Siri AI comes from Apple's support pages, and about each app from its App Store page (US unless we say otherwise), checked on 19 October 2026.

Who can use Siri AI today

Siri AI is the new Siri built on the next generation of Apple Intelligence. Apple's own page sets the limits:

  • It is a beta with a waitlist. You turn it on in Settings, Siri, Try Siri AI (Beta), and wait.
  • English only. “Siri AI is currently available in English”, and the device and Siri language must match.
  • Recent devices only: iPhone 15 Pro and later, iPhone 16 and later, iPhone Air, Macs and iPads with M1 or later.
  • Not on iPhone in the EU: “Siri AI is not available for iOS, iPadOS, or watchOS in the EU.” It is on macOS 27 and visionOS 27, in English.
  • Not in mainland China: it “will not currently work if your Apple Account region is in mainland China”, and Apple Intelligence as a whole does not work on devices bought there.

In practice that leaves out most iPhone owners in the world: anyone with an older iPhone, anyone who uses their phone in a language other than English, and everyone in the EU and China. In Russia there is a second wall: Russian is not one of the languages Apple Intelligence supports at all.

ChatGPT

The most rated AI app on the App Store, and the most direct replacement for “ask Siri anything”. Free to start, with Go, Plus and Pro plans in the app. A cloud service: its privacy label lists contact info, usage data and user content as linked to you.

Google Gemini

Free, from iOS 17.4, with paid tiers bundled with Google storage. If your mail, calendar and photos are at Google, it is the closest thing to the personal context Siri AI promises, inside Google's apps. Cloud only.

Claude

From Anthropic. Free to start, Pro at $20 a month. The best of these for writing, editing and working through long documents. Cloud only.

Vibe by Mistral

Formerly Le Chat, made by Mistral AI in Paris. Its privacy label separates contact info, which is linked to you, from usage data and user content, which it declares as not linked. Free, Vibe Pro at $14.99, iOS 16.4 or later. It is also sold in Russia and mainland China.

Pinku

Pinku does not replace Siri: it cannot read your messages, see your screen or act in other apps. It answers on the iPhone itself, with an open model you download once, so the conversation goes to no server and it works in airplane mode. No account. Chats never leave your device; Apple's privacy label lists only purchase history and an anonymous user ID, used for Pinku Pro purchases. Its interface is in eight languages, including Russian, Chinese, Japanese and Korean, and it runs on any iPhone 12 or later with iOS 18.6, with or without Apple Intelligence. The default model is free; Pinku Pro, an in-app purchase, adds larger models, custom instructions, dictation and read aloud. A phone-sized model is much weaker than the cloud apps above.

Locally AI by LM Studio

Free private offline chat: no login, Data Not Collected, iOS 18.1 or later, and it can also look at images. It is not sold in Russia or mainland China.

If you are in the EU

Everything above is available in the EU App Store. On an iPhone 15 Pro or later you keep the rest of Apple Intelligence: Apple says the new features across apps (advanced photo editing, the reimagined Image Playground, Safari's intelligent features) are available to EU users on iOS 27. On a Mac with macOS 27 Golden Gate, Siri AI is available in the EU if your device and Siri language are English.

If you are in Russia

On 19 October 2026 the Russian App Store does not sell ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or Locally AI. What it does have: Alice AI from Yandex (free, 4.8 from 53,071 ratings), DeepSeek (free, 3.9 from 9,770), Perplexity (free, 4.9 from 37,853) and Vibe by Mistral in the cloud; Pinku, PocketPal AI, Private LLM and Enclave offline. Of the offline apps, only Pinku has a Russian interface.

If you are in mainland China

Apple Intelligence does not work on iPhones bought in mainland China, and the Chinese App Store does not sell ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Locally AI or Enclave. The big local assistants are Doubao (ByteDance), DeepSeek, Kimi and Qwen (Alibaba), all free and all in the cloud. Offline, Pinku (with a Chinese interface), PocketPal AI and Private LLM are sold there.

Questions people ask

Is Siri AI available everywhere?

No. Apple says Siri AI is a beta, currently available in English only, not available on iPhone, iPad or Apple Watch in the EU, and not working for Apple Accounts in mainland China.

Is Siri AI available in the EU?

Not on iPhone. It is available on macOS 27 and visionOS 27 for EU accounts with the device and Siri language set to English. The other new Apple Intelligence features are available in the EU.

Does Siri AI work in Russian or Chinese?

Not yet. Apple says Siri AI is currently available in English. Russian is not among the languages Apple Intelligence supports at all; Chinese is, but Apple Intelligence does not work in mainland China.

What is the best Siri AI alternative on iPhone?

For asking anything, ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude; Vibe by Mistral for a European company. None of them reads your messages and acts across apps the way Siri AI does. For a private assistant that works offline, Pinku or Locally AI.

Is there an AI assistant that keeps my chats on my iPhone?

Yes. On-device apps such as Pinku and Locally AI run the model on the iPhone, so the conversation is not sent to a server. They are less capable than cloud assistants.
