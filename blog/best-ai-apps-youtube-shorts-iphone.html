Best AI apps to make YouTube Shorts on iPhone (2026)

The right app depends on what you start with: footage, a long video, a script or only an idea.

Maruta G · 2 November 2026 · ES · FR

Disclosure: we make Dora, number 3 on this list. Everything about the other apps comes from their own App Store pages. Written from App Store pages on 2 November 2026. We ran no quality test.

Most Shorts apps do one of four things. Editors polish clips you filmed. Clippers cut a long video into short ones. Script apps build a faceless short from text. Generators make a new clip from a description or photo. Choose by what you already have.

4. Vizard

5. VEED Lite

Auto edits and captions for clips up to 10 minutes, per its listing.

Can AI make a whole YouTube Short?

Script apps such as Faceless try to. You should still check facts and pacing, and YouTube asks creators to disclose realistic altered content.
