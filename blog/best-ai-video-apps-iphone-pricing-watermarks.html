Best AI video apps for iPhone: prices and watermarks (2026)

Six AI video apps for iPhone, and what each listing says about prices and watermarks. One of them is ours.

Maruta G · 25 November 2026 · Español

Short answer: generating video costs the developer money, so all six apps below charge inside the app, and the prices and watermark terms differ. Vivideo lists Pro and Max plans from $9.00 and says no watermark needs one of them. PixVerse Lite lists a no-watermark download feature in its release notes, an Elite Plan and credit packs. VideoGPT says its watermark was removed for subscribers and has the lowest credit packs, from $2.99. The Kling AI and Runway listings do not say anything about watermarks. Dora (Google Play) is ours: its listing promises no watermark, and it uses in-app purchases. Details are from the US App Store listings on 25 November 2026.

1. Vivideo: the clearest plan structure

Vivideo's listing is direct: the paid Pro and Max plans add credits, HD and 4K exports and removal of the watermark. That implies exports without a plan carry a watermark, though the page does not say it in those words.

Where others beat it: the listing prices start at $9.00 a week for Pro, which is high next to the other apps here.

2. PixVerse Lite: templates and a no-watermark download

PixVerse Lite's version notes mention a no-watermark download for finished videos and a set of templates for new users, plus daily points reward popups. The listing shows in-app purchases: an Elite Plan and credit packs.

Where others beat it: the listing does not say how many generations a credit pack buys, so you find out only after opening the app.

3. Kling AI: quality first, terms unclear

Kling AI sells credit packs from $5.00 for 330 credits and monthly plans from $10.00. The listing says nothing about watermarks, so check inside the app before you rely on it.

Where others beat it: if the main question is "what does the watermark policy say", other listings answer it more clearly.

4. Dora: several video models in one app

Dora is an app with AI video and image models for iPhone and Android. Pick a template or write an idea, add a photo, and the listing says the video comes back vertical, square or horizontal, with no watermark. We make Dora.

Where others beat it: if you want no watermark and a template to start from, it is an alternative to the apps above. It uses in-app purchases, and its listing does not publish a price list the way Vivideo does.

5. Runway: many models, the highest prices

Runway's listing offers credit packs and plans (Standard at $14.99 a month, 1000 credits for $10.00). It does not state whether exports carry a watermark.

Where others beat it: the prices are the highest of the group, and the watermark terms are unclear.

6. VideoGPT: cheap credits, watermark terms partly unclear

VideoGPT is iPhone only. Credit packs start at $2.99 for 100 credits and subscriptions at $6.99 a week. Its release notes say watermarks were removed for subscribers, but the listing does not say what other users get.

Where others beat it: it is a smaller feature set, and the watermark terms are not clear for users without a subscription.

How to choose

  • A clear plan structure with HD and 4K exports: Vivideo.
  • No watermark and a template to start from: PixVerse Lite or Dora (Google Play).
  • Several AI video models in one app: Dora (Google Play).
  • Cheapest credit packs listed: VideoGPT.
  • Check these first: Kling AI and Runway do not state watermark terms in their listings.

If you need a clean file for a client or a brand account, export one clip first and look at it.
