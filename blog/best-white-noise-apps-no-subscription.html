Best white noise and sleep sound apps without a subscription (2026)

Five apps you can use without a subscription: one price, a free tier, or free with ads. What each one costs and what it leaves out.

Maruta G · 25 November 2026 · Español

Short answer: White Noise Pro is the simplest way to own a noise machine: $2.99 once, 50 sounds and no ads. myNoise says on its page "one purchase, for life" with no subscription, and lets you shape each soundscape with sliders. If you only want free, White Noise Lite is free with ads, and Drop and Dark Noise are free with a limited set of sounds. Every app here can be used without a subscription; Drop and Dark Noise also list other Pro purchases, which the entries explain. One of these apps is ours, and the disclosure is at the end. Prices and details are from the US App Store listings on 25 November 2026.

"Not stated" means the App Store text does not say it, not that the app cannot do it. Listings show several prices under the same plan name, so these are the ones shown or described, and they can differ by country or offer.

1. White Noise Pro: one price, no ads

TMSOFT's paid version of the White Noise app costs $2.99 and lists 50 looped stereo sounds, an advanced alarm and timer that fades audio in and out, timed playlists, a Mix Pad editor to combine sounds, and no advertisements. Its page lists no in-app purchases.

Where others beat it: you pay before you can try it (White Noise Lite is the free version to test with), and its listing mentions no widgets.

2. myNoise: you shape the sound, and pay once

Every soundscape has its own sliders, so you can change which parts of the sound you hear. The app is free to download, with Full Access at $19.99 and All You Can Hear at $14.99; its page describes "one purchase, for life" with no subscription, and offline listening.

Where others beat it: its listing mentions no sleep timer.

3. Dark Noise: a free tier with 8 sounds

The free tier has 8 sounds, a timer that fades noise out before you wake, Siri Shortcuts and interactive widgets, including Lock Screen widgets. Dark Noise Pro unlocks all 50+ sounds and custom mixes, and a Pro Lifetime purchase costs $49.99. The free and Pro split comes from release notes dated 2023, so check the app.

Where others beat it: the listing also shows other Pro purchases at $0.99, $2.99 and $19.99 without saying whether they are subscriptions, so confirm what you are buying before you confirm the payment.

4. Drop: a free tier with no ads

The app is free with no ads, no account and nine free sounds, including white noise, a crackling fire, a forest, a heartbeat, a lake and a coffee shop. It works offline, keeps playing with the screen locked and has a sleep timer from 15 minutes to 8 hours with a 30-second fade-out. Drop Pro unlocks the other sounds, including rain, ocean waves and pink and brown noise. Pro can be bought once as Drop Pro Lifetime, so Drop fits this page, but the listing also shows Pro Monthly at $4.99 and Pro Access at $14.99, and our page says Pro is also sold by subscription, so pick the lifetime option if you want no subscription.

Where others beat it: Drop plays one sound at a time with no equaliser, has no alarm, and the lifetime price shows at three different amounts in the listing. White Noise Pro and White Noise Lite have an alarm, and myNoise has sliders.

5. White Noise Lite: free, with ads

TMSOFT's free version has a timer that fades audio in and out, a Mix Pad editor, background playback while you use other apps and AirPlay. Its page says "upgrade to the full version to remove advertising", and lists no in-app purchases.

Where others beat it: it shows ads, and its page claims 50+ sounds while its sound catalogue lists 40.

How to choose

  • Pay once, ad-free, and be done: White Noise Pro.
  • Pay once and shape the sound: myNoise.
  • Free, with a few sounds and no ads: Drop or Dark Noise.
  • Free and you accept ads: White Noise Lite.
