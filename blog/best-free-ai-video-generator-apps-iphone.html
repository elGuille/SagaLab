Best free AI video generator apps for iPhone (2026)

Six AI video apps for iPhone, and what each listing says about free use and watermarks. One of them is ours.

Maruta G · 25 November 2026 · Español

Short answer: no AI video app is fully free, because generating video costs the developer money. All six below charge inside the app, and what a free start gives you differs. Vivideo says the first video is free and that no watermark needs Pro or Max. PixVerse Lite lists a no-watermark download feature in its release notes and limited free templates for new users. VideoGPT says its watermark was removed for subscribers. The Kling AI and Runway listings do not say anything about watermarks. Dora is ours: its listing promises no watermark, and it uses in-app purchases. Details are from the US App Store listings on 25 November 2026.

Free tiers change often, so test an export before you build anything on it. What follows is only what each listing says today.

We did not test free exports ourselves, so the table repeats the listings, not our results.

1. Vivideo: the clearest free first video

Vivideo's listing is direct: the first video is free, you can earn credits with simple tasks, and the paid Pro and Max plans add credits, HD and 4K exports and removal of the watermark. That implies free exports carry a watermark, though the page does not say it in those words.

Where others beat it: the listing prices start at $9.00 a week for Pro, which is high next to the other apps here.

2. PixVerse Lite: templates and a no-watermark download

PixVerse Lite's version notes mention a no-watermark download for finished videos and a limited set of free templates for new users, plus daily points reward popups. It is free to install with in-app purchases, and the listing shows an Elite Plan and credit packs.

Where others beat it: the listing does not say how many free generations you get, so you find out only after opening the app.

3. Kling AI: quality first, free terms unclear

Kling AI is free to install, with credit packs from $5.00 for 330 credits and monthly plans from $10.00. The listing says nothing about watermarks or free credits, so check inside the app before you rely on it.

Where others beat it: if the main question is "what do I get for free", other listings answer it more clearly.

4. Dora: several video models in one app

Dora is an app with AI video and image models for iPhone and Android. Pick a template or write an idea, add a photo, and the listing says the video comes back vertical, square or horizontal, with no watermark. We make Dora.

Where others beat it: if you want no watermark and a template to start from, it is an alternative to the apps above. It uses in-app purchases, and its listing makes no offer of a first video like Vivideo does.

5. Runway: free to start, many models

Runway's listing says "Free to start" and offers credit packs and plans (Standard at $14.99 a month, 1000 credits for $10.00). It does not state what the free start includes or whether exports carry a watermark.

Where others beat it: the paid prices are the highest of the group, and the free terms are unclear.

6. VideoGPT: cheap credits, watermark for non-subscribers unclear

VideoGPT is iPhone only. Credit packs start at $2.99 for 100 credits and subscriptions at $6.99 a week. Its release notes say watermarks were removed for subscribers, but the listing does not say what free users get.

Where others beat it: it is a smaller feature set, and the watermark terms are not clear for free users.

How to choose

  • A free first clip to try the idea: Vivideo.
  • No watermark and a template to start from: PixVerse Lite or Dora.
  • Several AI video models in one app: Dora.
  • Cheapest credit packs listed: VideoGPT.
  • Check these first: Kling AI and Runway do not state free terms in their listings.

If you need a clean file for a client or a brand account, export one clip on the free tier first and look at it.
