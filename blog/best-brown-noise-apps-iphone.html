Best brown noise apps for iPhone (2026)

Four brown noise apps for iPhone: what each costs, which show ads, and what their timers do. One of them is ours.

Maruta G · 25 November 2026 · Español

Short answer: Noise Machine is the most complete of the four: brown, pink, green and white noise, a sleep timer and lock screen controls, but it shows ads and sells several purchases. Brownoise is the plainest: one brown noise you can tune, with a lifetime purchase listed at $4.99. Brown Noise by Sleep Sounds lists no purchases at all. Drop is ours, and brown noise is part of its Pro sounds, not the free nine. Prices and details are from the US App Store listings on 25 November 2026.

Brown noise is a deep, low-frequency sound, softer than white noise. This page makes no health claims about any sound. For the difference between the colours see brown noise vs pink noise vs white noise.

"Not mentioned" means the listing does not say it, not that the app does not do it. Privacy labels are written by each developer and Apple states it has not verified them.

1. Noise Machine: four noise colours in one app

From Zipo Apps, the listing offers brown, pink, green and white noise, a sleep timer that fades the noise out, lock screen controls, Siri, and playback to speakers, AirPods or HomePod. It can mix with audio from other apps. It runs on iPhone and iPad, and the listing also shows Mac and Apple Vision.

Where others beat it: it contains advertising and sells many purchases, so check what you are buying. Brownoise and Brown Noise by Sleep Sounds describe no ads.

2. Brownoise: one sound you can tune

This app does one thing: brown noise with sliders for intensity, frequency and gain, and a timer. It is free with a single Lifetime purchase at $4.99, and the listing says it is only for iPhone.

Where others beat it: its page does not say whether it plays with the screen locked or works offline, and it has no other sounds.

3. Drop: brown noise in Pro, with a free tier

Drop is free with nine sounds, no ads and no account. Pro unlocks the other sounds, including pink and brown noise. It works offline, keeps playing with the screen locked, has a sleep timer from 15 minutes to 8 hours with a 30-second fade-out, and runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Pro is sold once as Drop Pro Lifetime and also by subscription.

Where others beat it: you cannot try brown noise in the free tier. Drop plays one sound at a time with no mixer and has no sliders for the noise itself, which Brownoise has. We make Drop.

4. Brown Noise by Sleep Sounds: free, with a bear

A free iPhone app with brown noise, a sleep timer with a gentle chime, a night mode, and a small bear that falls asleep on screen. Its listing shows no in-app purchases and the developer label says data is not collected.

Where others beat it: one user review says the end-of-timer alarm cannot be turned off, and the listing mentions no lock screen controls or mixing.

How to choose

  • Several noise colours and Siri: Noise Machine, if you accept ads.
  • Just brown noise, tunable, small one-time price: Brownoise.
  • Free with no purchase to look at: Brown Noise by Sleep Sounds.
  • Also want other calming sounds, offline, no ads: Drop, and pay for Pro if you want brown noise.
