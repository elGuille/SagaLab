Best AI video generator apps for Android (2026)

Ten apps that turn a photo or a few words into a short video on Android, which of them have a real free tier, and who each one is for. One of them is ours.

Maruta G · 25 November 2026 · Español

Short answer: there is no single best one, and no app here gives unlimited free video. Of the apps we checked, the only free plan we could confirm on the maker's own site is Runway's: $0 a month with 125 one-time credits, which are not renewed. Third-party guides report free daily credits for Kling AI and PixVerse, but their Google Play listings do not say so and we could not read the makers' pricing pages, so we do not state numbers. The listings of Hailuo AI, Vidu and Dreamina say nothing about a free tier. Google Gemini and Dora have no free video. Runway is the broadest choice, Kling AI is built for longer and sharper clips, PixVerse for templates, and invideo and CapCut for editing.

Disclosure: we make Dora, one of the apps below. Saga Labs, the studio that runs this site, builds and sells it. It is not placed first, and the last section says what it does and does not do. Every fact about the other apps comes from its Google Play listing (US store, English), read on 25 November 2026, or from the maker's own site where we say so. We do not quote star ratings or review counts.

Which apps have a real free tier

  1. Confirmed: Runway. Its pricing page lists a Free plan at $0 a month with 125 one-time credits "to explore Runway's AI tools" (read on 25 November 2026). The credits are a one-time allowance, not a daily or monthly one, so they run out. Paid plans on the same page start at $15 a month, or $12 a month billed annually.
  2. Reported, but we could not confirm: Kling AI and PixVerse. Third-party guides report free daily credits for both, usually with a watermark, and they disagree on the amounts. The makers' pricing pages did not load usable text for us, and neither Google Play listing states a free amount.
  3. Not stated: Hailuo AI, Vidu, Dreamina. Hailuo's listing mentions an optional subscription for some advanced features, which suggests a base tier without saying what it includes. Open any of these apps, sign up, and read what the free account allows before you pay.
  4. No free video: Google Gemini and Dora. Gemini is free to install and chat with, but its listing offers video with Gemini Omni only to Google AI Plus, Pro, Ultra and Workspace users. Dora is paid only.
  5. Unclear: invideo and CapCut. Neither listing states what is free. CapCut's listing describes an editor, not prompt-to-video generation.

Runway: the broadest choice, and the one free plan we could confirm

Runway's listing puts several well known models in one app: it names Seedance 2.5, Kling 3 Pro, Gen-4.5, Nano Banana Pro and Nano Banana 2. It does text to video, image to video and video to video, keeps characters and places consistent across shots, and its Runway Agent plans, generates and assembles a whole video from a description. Work syncs with Runway on a computer. The free plan is small and one-time, so treat it as a trial. Best for people who want to pick the model for each job.

Kling AI: longer, sharper clips

Kling AI runs Kling's own models. Its listing says it generates up to 30 seconds in native 1080p or cinema-grade 4K, and that Video Extension reaches up to 3 minutes. It also makes images, and has a community feed where you can clone a piece you like and try the idea yourself. Best for people who care most about motion quality or need a clip longer than a few seconds.

PixVerse: templates and trends

PixVerse's listing describes an app powered by PixVerse V6 with access to outside models, built around templates: AI dance, transformations, beauty and miniature styles. It also lists Motion Control (a character image plus a reference motion video), lip sync for talking avatars, video extension, and upscaling up to 4K. The listing says the app contains ads and offers in-app purchases. Best for people who follow trends and want a result in a few taps.

Hailuo AI: from MiniMax, simple and direct

Hailuo AI, published on Google Play by MiniMax AI, turns text or an image into video, and its Subject Reference keeps a character consistent across scenes. The listing is short and does not name models or durations. It notes that some advanced features need an optional subscription. Best for people who want straightforward text to video or image to video. Check the publisher name before you install: several unofficial apps use similar names.

Vidu: sound and community remixes

Vidu, from ShengShu AI, turns text into video with automatically matched background music, voiceover and sound effects, lets you remix ideas from the community, and accepts any image as a reference for style or characters. Best for short clips where you want sound generated with the picture.

Dreamina AI: ByteDance's image and video app

Dreamina covers images, posters, video from text or an image, reference-guided generation and smart editing, with a community feed. The listing is general and does not name video models. Best for people who also want image and poster generation in the same app.

Google Gemini: video only on a Google plan

Gemini is Google's assistant. Its listing says you can turn ideas into cinematic videos with Gemini Omni for Google AI Plus, Pro, Ultra and Workspace users, and generate and edit images with Nano Banana 2. It is the choice if you already pay for one of those Google plans. It is not a free way to make video.

invideo: when you also need to edit

invideo combines generation and editing. Its Agent Two turns a description into a video, every AI edit stays editable on a multitrack timeline, it offers more than 200 models for images, video, voice, sound and music, and teams can edit together. Best for ads, explainers and YouTube videos that need more than one clip.

CapCut: the editor, not a generator

CapCut's listing describes an editor: trimming, keyframes, multitrack timeline, auto captions, text to speech and background removal. It does not describe generating video from a prompt. Use it to finish clips made in any app on this list.

From the team at Saga Labs

We make Dora. On Android it is listed on Google Play as "Photo to Video: AI Templates", and the same account and coins work on the web at Dora and on iPhone. You pick a template that puts your photo into a video (the listing mentions talk-to-camera vlogs, time travel and 80s looks), or write your own idea, add a photo or not, and leave the model on Auto or choose one. Motion Control copies the moves of a short video onto the person in your photo. Every template and the Create button show the coin cost before you start, and if a creation fails the coins go back to your balance. Dora also works inside Claude and ChatGPT through the same account.

What it does not do: it has no free generations. The Google Play listing describes a weekly subscription that adds coins every week, with coin packs on top (pack coins do not expire), and it mentions no free trial. It is not an editor, so there is no timeline and no publishing to social networks; you save the file and take it to CapCut or invideo. The iPhone listing says more about video quality than the Google Play one does, so on this page we describe only what the Play listing says. Facts about every other app come from its own listing, like Dora's. Get it on Google Play, on the App Store (Google Play) or on the web.

How to choose

  • The most models, and a free plan to try: Runway.
  • Longer, sharper clips: Kling AI.
  • Templates and trends: PixVerse.
  • Video with generated sound: Vidu.
  • You already pay for a Google AI plan: Gemini.
  • A finished ad or explainer with editing and a team: invideo.
  • Editing what you already made: CapCut.
  • Templates that put your photo into a video, or video from inside Claude or ChatGPT, and you accept a paid plan: Dora.

Questions people ask

What is the best AI video generator app for Android?

It depends on the job. Runway is the broadest, with many models and an agent. Kling AI is built for longer clips in 1080p or 4K. PixVerse is built around templates. invideo and CapCut are for editing. Dora, made by Saga Labs, who wrote this page, fits people who want templates that put their photo into a video, or who work inside Claude or ChatGPT, on a paid plan.

Third-party guides report free daily credits for Kling AI and PixVerse, but their Google Play listings do not say so and we did not confirm amounts; the listings of Hailuo AI, Vidu and Dreamina are silent. Can I turn a photo into a video on Android?

Yes. The Google Play listings of Runway, PixVerse, Hailuo AI, Dreamina, Kling AI and Dora all describe making video from a photo or image. Dora's listing describes templates that put your photo into a video and a Create screen where you add one photo and describe the movement.

Does Dora have a free version on Android?

No. Dora's Google Play listing describes a weekly subscription that adds coins every week, plus coin packs, and mentions no free trial or free generations. In-app purchases are listed from $4.99 to $144.99.

Are Pika and Luma Dream Machine on Android?

We found no Google Play listing for either on 25 November 2026, and Pika's own site links only an iOS app. Check the company's site for current availability.
