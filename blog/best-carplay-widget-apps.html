Best CarPlay widget apps (2026)

Apple's own way to add widgets first, then five widget apps compared by what their listings say you can put on the car screen.

Maruta G · 25 November 2026 · Español

Short answer: CarPlay widgets are built into iOS 26 and later, and you add them from your iPhone's settings, not from the car. Apple's own widgets and the widgets of apps you already have appear there too. A widget app is worth it when you want something the built-in ones do not offer, such as your own photo, a countdown or a parking timer. Below: how Apple's route works, then five apps compared. We make CarScreen, number 3; see the last section. Facts about every app come from its US App Store listing, checked on 25 November 2026.

Apple's own option: widgets from any app

Apple's newsroom announcement for iOS 26 says that iOS 26 brings widgets to CarPlay. Apple Support gives the steps: when you are not driving, open Settings on your iPhone, tap General, tap CarPlay, tap your car, tap Widgets, then tap Add Widgets and follow the onscreen instructions. The number of widget stacks you see depends on your vehicle's CarPlay screen, and the same page covers rearranging or removing widgets and turning on Smart Rotate or Widget Suggestions.

Apple's CarPlay page for developers says widgets appear to the left of the CarPlay Dashboard, support touch on touchscreen vehicles, and that if an app already has a small widget or Live Activity it appears in CarPlay with no changes, unless the developer marks it as not suitable for the car. So any app with a small widget may already be an option, before you install anything new.

We ranked on one question: how many different things each app's listing says you can put on the CarPlay widget screen. We did not install them in a car. All five are free to download; prices and plans change by country, so we do not quote them.

1. CarMood: the most widget types

The longest widget list in this group: clock, date, weather, speed and trip stats, your photos, your car's logo, license plates of 40 countries, flags, a parking spot map, fuel range, moon phase, countdowns and seasonal designs, with one to three widget slots and a separate set for every car in its Virtual Garage. It also adds one-tap themes, sounds that play when CarPlay connects, a logbook for trips and fuel, and navigation. Its listing says Pro unlocks all designs, themes, sounds, AI tools, navigation, Drive Mode and the full logbook. If widgets are what you want, it offers the most choice, with a lot of other features around them.

2. CARFACE: photo and vehicle widgets with lyrics

Widgets with photos, vehicle visuals and layouts, plus real-time lyrics for Apple Music and Spotify, custom connect and disconnect sounds, and a section for M3U and M3U8 playlists. Its listing names categories such as pets and animals, daily affirmations and seasonal themes. It is a good fit if you also want lyrics on the car screen.

3. CarScreen: a few practical widgets plus parked video

Our app, described in the last section. Placed third because its widget list is shorter than the first two.

4. Car Connect (CarPlay Widgets): simple widgets and trip tracking

Photo, clock, countdown and text widgets with adjustable colors and layouts, sounds you can attach to a CarPlay connection with an Apple Shortcuts automation, and GPS trip tracking. Its listing says CarPlay widgets need iOS 26 or later and a compatible vehicle or head unit, and that GPS speed and trip statistics are estimates. It is a different app from another one named Car Connect on the App Store, so check the developer name, Ivanna Koryliak.

5. CarTheme: design your own widget

An editor for colors, backgrounds, spacing and icons, with several layout styles and widgets that refresh at the system's intervals. Its listing says it is compatible with iOS 26 and iOS 27 and that optional subscriptions unlock more styles. It names no specific widget types, which is why it is last on a ranking by variety, though it may suit you if you prefer to design the layout yourself.

How to choose

  • You only want a clock or the weather: check the widgets of apps you already have first; Apple says any app's small widget can appear in CarPlay.
  • The most widget types and themes: CarMood.
  • Photos with lyrics: CARFACE.
  • A parking timer, a countdown and your photo, with your own videos for when you are parked: CarScreen.
  • Designing the look yourself: CarTheme.

Questions people ask

How do I add widgets to CarPlay?

When you are not driving, open Settings on your iPhone, tap General, CarPlay, your car, then Widgets and Add Widgets, and follow the onscreen instructions. Apple Support says the number of widget stacks depends on your vehicle's CarPlay screen.

Which iOS version has CarPlay widgets?

Apple's iOS 26 announcement says iOS 26 brings widgets to CarPlay. The apps compared here list iOS 26.0 or later as their App Store minimum.

Do I need a widget app for CarPlay?

Not always. Apple's developer page says an app's small widget or Live Activity appears in CarPlay with no changes unless the developer marks it as unsuitable for the car. A widget app adds designs such as your own photo, countdowns or a parking timer.

About CarScreen

We make CarScreen (its current US App Store name is "M3U IPTV Player: CarScreen"), number 3 above. Its widgets are your own photo, the clock and date, a countdown to a trip or any date, your car's name and license plate, your parking place with the time elapsed, and your own quote or message, in 6 colour themes, and the parking timer also runs as a Live Activity on the iPhone. To add them, use Apple's path above: Settings, General, CarPlay, your car, Widgets.

What it does not do: it has fewer widget types than CarMood or CARFACE, and its listing names no weather, speed or trip widgets. Its main job is playing your own videos and M3U playlists on the car screen while parked, which needs a car that supports AirPlay video, so widgets are the lighter half of the app. It needs iOS 26.0 or later.
