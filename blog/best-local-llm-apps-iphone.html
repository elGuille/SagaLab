Best local LLM apps for iPhone (2026)

For a local LLM on iPhone, Locally AI is the cheapest start and Private LLM the best one-time buy. Pinku is ours, fourth, and paid.

Maruta G · 24 November 2026

Written from App Store pages on 24 November 2026. We ran no speed or quality test.

A local LLM is a language model that runs on your own device instead of a company's server. On iPhone that means Llama, Gemma, Qwen and similar open models, downloaded once and run on the phone's chip. This page ranks six apps by which models and formats they run and what they cost. Prices, requirements and ratings checked on the US App Store on 24 November 2026. Ratings rest on very different numbers of reviews.

1. Locally AI by LM Studio

Its listing names Llama 3.2, Gemma 2 and 3, Qwen 3 and DeepSeek, run on Apple MLX, with image analysis and no login. It shows no in-app purchases, so it is the cheapest way in. The listing does not describe a custom-instruction or document feature.

2. Private LLM

The listing says it is a one-time purchase with no subscription and Family Sharing. It names the longest list of model families here: Llama 2, 3.1 and 3.2, Gemma 2 and 3, Phi-3, Mistral 7B, Qwen 2.5 and 3 and StableLM 3B, with Siri and Shortcuts and custom system prompts. Its US rating is 4.1.

3. PocketPal AI

You download model weights in GGUF format, and the app searches Hugging Face for them. That makes it the most open of the six: if a model exists as a GGUF, you can try it. It also runs on the oldest iOS in this list. The listing shows in-app purchases, and you pick and judge models yourself.

4. Pinku

Pinku is a paid app: to chat you need Pinku Pro, which is a one-time $12.99 purchase or a yearly plan. Its listing names 12 models on Apple MLX: Qwen3, Llama 3.2, Gemma 3, 3n and 4, SmolLM3 and LFM2.5, from 0.4 to 4.7 GB. It lists only the models your device has memory for and checks the available memory before loading one, so a model that does not fit is refused rather than crashing the app. You can attach a PDF or text file and ask about it, and start a chat from Siri and Shortcuts. It runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Where others win: Locally AI costs nothing, PocketPal and Enclave offer far more models, and Private LLM costs less. Pinku has no way to load a model outside its list, its models are clearly weaker than frontier cloud models, and it cannot browse the web.

Pinku is in the App Store: Pinku on the App Store. More on its page: aisagalab.com/pinku.

5. Enclave

Hundreds of open models, voice chat with on-device speech, document chat and custom assistants. A Pro subscription at $9.99 adds cloud models, which are not local. Its listing requires iOS 27, so it is out of reach for iPhones that stay on an older version.

6. Noema

The widest list of formats: GGUF, MLX, ExecuTorch, Core ML and Apple's own models. It shows memory-fit guidance before you download, and imports PDFs and EPUBs. It also offers a local API for other apps on your network and optional web search and remote providers, which you turn on yourself.

Local LLMs on a Mac

The same apps often have a Mac version. From the requirement lines in their listings: Private LLM and Pinku need macOS 14, PocketPal AI lists macOS 12 on an Apple M1 chip or later, Locally AI and Noema need macOS 26, and Enclave needs macOS 27. We did not test which are native Mac apps. A Mac has more memory than a phone, so it can hold larger models than an iPhone can; our model-fit tool shows which of Pinku's models fit each device. Ollama and LM Studio are downloaded from their own sites, not the App Store; we compared what they send over the network in our Mac network test.

How to choose

  • You want to try a local LLM at no cost: Locally AI or Noema.
  • You want to pay once and have many named models: Private LLM.
  • You want a model nobody curated for you: PocketPal AI.
  • You want a short list that your phone's memory is checked against, and you accept paying for Pro: Pinku.
  • You also want cloud models in the same app: Enclave.

Disclosure: we make Pinku, number 4 on this list, and say where other apps beat it. Everything about the other apps comes from their own App Store pages.

What is a local LLM?

A language model that runs on your own device rather than on a company's server. On iPhone the apps here download open models such as Llama, Gemma and Qwen once and run them on the phone.

Does a local LLM need internet?

To download the model, yes. After that these apps run it on the device. PocketPal AI's listing says you connect to the internet to download the model weights, then chat offline.

How big a model can an iPhone run?

It depends on the phone's memory. Pinku's models range from 0.4 to 4.7 GB and the app lists only those your device can hold. Other apps leave the choice to you.

Is a local LLM as good as ChatGPT?

No. A model of a few gigabytes on a phone is clearly weaker than a frontier cloud model. What you gain is that the conversation stays on the device.
