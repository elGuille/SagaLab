Best crypto price widget apps for iPhone (2026)

Five apps compared on the widgets themselves: where they can go, what they show and what you pay for them.

Maruta G · 25 November 2026 · Español

Short answer: Crypto Pro lists the most places to put a price: Home Screen, Lock Screen, StandBy, Apple Watch and a Mac menu bar widget. CoinStats is next, with Home Screen, Lock Screen and Apple Watch, plus wallet and exchange sync. If you only want the price of a few coins on the Home Screen with no payment, CoinGecko and Crypton do that for free. One of these apps is ours, and the disclosure is at the end. Details are from the US App Store listings on 25 November 2026.

1. Crypto Pro: the most places to put a price

Its description lists customisable theme, size and refresh for widgets, a widget for the Mac menu bar, Home Screen placement and Apple Watch complications for every watch face; its version history mentions Lock Screen widgets, StandBy support and extra-large widgets. It shows prices, charts, news and portfolios. Premium is a paid plan with a 7-day trial that adds exchange API, wallet imports and technical analysis data. Its developer text describes no account.

Where others beat it: wallet and exchange import is a Premium feature, while CoinStats's page names 300+ wallets and exchanges.

2. CoinStats: widgets plus wallets and exchanges

Its page says your portfolio and live prices go on the Home Screen, the Lock Screen and the wrist, and recent release notes mention Home Screen and Lock Screen widget fixes and watch complications. It also sets price, volume or market cap alerts and syncs 300+ wallets and exchanges.

Where others beat it: the plans are the hardest to read: the listing shows Pro and Premium with several prices, and the text quotes another pair.

3. Delta: Net Worth, Top Movers and Watchlist widgets

Delta's release notes name three widgets: Net Worth, Top Movers and Watchlist. The listing does not say whether they sit on the Home Screen or the Lock Screen. It also has price alerts. The free plan covers up to 10 assets.

Where others beat it: the widget placements are not documented in the listing, so check before you subscribe.

4. CoinGecko: a free Home Screen widget

The page describes a crypto widget that tracks prices and your portfolio on the Home Screen, and the app sets price alerts and large-mover alerts. Premium is a paid, ad-free plan with a free trial; the listing shows $9.90 a month and $99.90 a year.

Where others beat it: the listing mentions no Lock Screen or Apple Watch widgets.

5. Crypton: two widgets, and nothing else to set up

Two Home Screen widgets: Coin List, where you choose the coins, in small, medium and large, and Portfolio, with value, today's change and biggest holdings. Prices cover the top 250 coins plus search for any coin listed on CoinGecko. It is free with no in-app purchases, and the account is optional, used only to sync a portfolio. Prices are refreshed every few hours, not in real time, according to our own product notes.

Where others beat it: the listing mentions no Lock Screen widget, no Apple Watch, no price alerts and no wallet or exchange connection. It only tracks: it does not buy, sell or store crypto.

How to choose

  • Lock Screen, StandBy, Apple Watch or Mac: Crypto Pro, or CoinStats if you also want wallets synced.
  • Widgets and price alerts: Crypto Pro, CoinStats or CoinGecko.
  • The cheapest option that works: Crypton or the free tier of CoinGecko.

About Crypton

The trade-offs are the ones listed above: only the top 250 coins plus search, no Lock Screen widget, no Apple Watch, no price alerts and no wallet or exchange sync, so Crypto Pro and CoinStats do more.
