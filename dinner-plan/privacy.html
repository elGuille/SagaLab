Dinner Plan: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-14
Developer: Saga AI Research Labs
App: Dinner Plan (iPhone)

1. Summary

Dinner Plan has no account and does not ask for your name or email. To build your weekly dinners it sends your food preferences to our server, which uses an AI model to suggest dishes and recipes. It has no advertising and no analytics.

2. What the app sends to make your plan

When you create or change a plan, or open a recipe, the app sends to Saga's server:

  • the store you picked, your weekly budget, how many nights you cook and the cuisines you like;
  • for each household member: an internal ID, their diet, allergies and the foods they don't want. Member names or labels are never sent;
  • the dishes already in your plan, so they are not repeated;
  • an anonymous app user ID, used to check your subscription and to limit abuse.

Our server passes the food preferences to a third-party AI model provider to generate the dishes and recipe steps, and returns the result to the app. The app asks for your consent before the first request. We do not use this data for advertising and we do not sell it.

3. What stays on your iPhone

Your plan, grocery list, saved recipes and settings are stored on your device.

4. Purchases

Dinner Plan is a subscription sold through Apple. Apple processes the payment. The app uses RevenueCat to know what you have bought; RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt and an anonymous app user ID, not your name, email or food preferences. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

5. Prices

Grocery costs are estimates based on typical prices at the store you choose. Dinner Plan is not affiliated with any store.

6. Children

Dinner Plan is not directed at children under 13.

7. Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove what it stores. To ask us about data processed on our server, write to the contact below.

8. Changes

We will update this page if this changes. The effective date shows the latest version.
