Subzy privacy: your subscriptions stay on your iPhone

Subzy keeps the list of subscriptions you enter on your iPhone. It does not connect to your bank, does not read your email and has no account. This page lists everything the app sends over the network, and why.

Updated 10 November 2026. This page covers the Subzy app (Subzy: Cancel Subscriptions); the general Saga Labs privacy policy covers our website and other apps.

Your subscriptions

  • The subscriptions you add (name, price, billing date, notes) are stored only on your device. We never receive them.
  • Subzy does not connect to your bank, card or email, and it cannot see the subscriptions of other apps: you add them yourself.
  • There is no account and no sign-up. Deleting the app deletes your list.
  • If you export a CSV file, it is created on the device and you choose where to send it.

What the app sends over the network

  • Subzy Pro purchases. Payments go through Apple. We use RevenueCat to know whether you have Pro: it receives your purchase history and an anonymous app user ID, never your list of subscriptions.
  • Cancel links. When you tap a cancel link, the page opens in Safari or in the service's app. Subzy sends nothing to that site; the site sees you as it would if you opened it yourself.
  • Contact. If you tap Contact, your email app opens a message to us. Nothing is sent unless you send that email.

What stays on the device

  • Your settings and reminder preferences.
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on the device.
  • The widget reads a small summary (next charges and monthly total) that the app saves on the device.

No ads, no tracking

Subzy has no advertising and no analytics or tracking SDK. Apple's App Store privacy label for Subzy lists only purchase history and an identifier used for Pro.
