Seedance vs Kling: which AI video model to use (2026)

What ByteDance and Kling say their models do, what each costs on the dates we checked, and which one fits which job. We make Dora, which offers both.

Maruta G · 25 November 2026

Short answer: there is no winner on every job, and we have not run a same-prompt test of the two, so we do not rank their picture quality. Use Seedance (ByteDance) when you want a clip built from several references at once, up to nine images, three video clips and three audio clips per ByteDance, or, in Dora, the lower-cost Seedance 2 Mini for a short clip with sound. Use Kling when you want Kling's own multi-shot control, a choice of 1080p or 720p, and a price list per second of video. Both state clips of up to 15 seconds with native audio. In Dora, which offers both, the Seedance models are the default and the Kling that is listed is the older 2.6.

Which to use for which job

  • Animate one photo, cheaply, with sound: Seedance 2 Mini. It is Dora's default, takes one photo as the first frame and is the lowest default cost of the Seedance and Kling models in the table above.
  • The best Seedance quality Dora offers: Seedance 2, at a higher cost. Dora itself says it is slower and has no 1080p.
  • A clip assembled from many references (images, video and audio together): Seedance 2.0 as ByteDance describes it, up to 9 images, 3 videos and 3 audio clips. We did not test how any platform exposes all of those inputs.
  • Several planned shots, or 1080p, with a published price per second: Kling VIDEO 3.0 on Kling's own platform.
  • Copy a dance or action onto a person in a photo: Kling 2.6 Motion Control in Dora.

Questions people ask

What is the difference between Seedance and Kling?

Seedance is ByteDance's video model family and Kling is Kuaishou's. By their makers' own pages, Seedance 2.0 takes up to 9 images, 3 video clips and 3 audio clips plus text, while Kling VIDEO 3.0 offers 1080p or 720p, native audio and two multi-shot modes. Both state clips up to 15 seconds.

Which is better, Seedance or Kling?

We have not run a same-prompt test, so we do not rank picture quality. Seedance suits clips built from many references and short, cheap animation with sound; Kling suits planned multi-shot clips and has a published price per second.

How much does Kling cost?

Kling's VIDEO 3.0 guide lists, per second: Native Audio 12 credits at 1080p and 9 at 720p; No Native Audio 8 at 1080p and 6 at 720p; Voice Control adds 2. A 5-second Native Audio 1080p video costs 60 credits (25 November 2026). We did not find the money value of a credit.

How much does Seedance cost in Dora?

On 25 November 2026 Dora's connector lists Seedance 2 Mini at 31 coins by default (16 to 99 depending on settings) and Seedance 2 at 152 coins by default (76 to 1,224). Dora has paid plans only and shows the cost before you generate.

Can I use Seedance and Kling in the same app?

In Dora you can use the Seedance 2 models and Kling 2.6, which Dora lists as an image-to-video model and offers in its connector as Motion Control. Kling VIDEO 3.0 is not listed there.
