ChatGPT video generator cost: what a video or image really costs, model by model

ChatGPT has no video model of its own since Sora closed, so a video made in a chat is paid to the plugin that makes it, not to OpenAI. Here is what that costs with Dora AI Video, model by model, with the numbers read on 2 November 2026.

Maruta G · 2 November 2026 · Leer en español

Short answer: with the Dora AI Video plugin, an image costs 1 to 18 coins and a video 15 to 76 coins at the default settings. A coin costs about 1 cent on the weekly plan and 2 cents on the yearly one, so a default video (Seedance 2 Mini, 31 coins) is about $0.31 to $0.62, and the priciest default (Seedance 2, 76 coins) about $0.76 to $1.52. Your ChatGPT plan pays for none of it.

Who you pay when ChatGPT makes a video

OpenAI closed the Sora app in April 2026 and its API in September 2026, so ChatGPT itself no longer makes video. Video in a chat comes from a plugin in ChatGPT Plugins, and each plugin bills on your account with its own service, in its own credits. That is why the question “how much does a ChatGPT video cost” has no single answer: it depends on the plugin and on the model it uses. This page gives the numbers for one plugin, Dora AI Video, because they are public and we could read them on the same day. For what the other video plugins do, see the best ChatGPT plugins for video and images; we have not checked their prices for this page.

What a plan buys, in clips

  • 500 coins (one week weekly, or one month yearly): about 16 default videos (Seedance 2 Mini, 31 coins), 25 Grok Imagine 1.5 clips or 50 default images (Nano Banana 2, 10 coins).
  • The first week's 300 coins: about 9 default videos or 30 default images.

These are at the default settings. Longer or sharper clips use more coins each.

No coins, or a clip that fails

  • No coins: nothing is made and nothing is charged. ChatGPT says that generating needs coins and links to the page with your balance and the plans; once you have coins, ask again in the same chat (Dora's plugin guide).
  • A clip that fails: the coins go back to your balance, and the plugin tells ChatGPT the generation failed and the credits were refunded.
  • Coins and credits are the same thing; Dora's pages use both words.

Step by step

  1. Add the plugin. In ChatGPT, open Plugins, search for Dora AI Video, install it and sign in with a Dora account (Apple, Google or email).
  2. Ask for the prices first. Write “list Dora's models and what each costs in coins”. ChatGPT reads the list from the plugin.
  3. Ask for the clip and a quote. Describe the video and add “tell me the cost first and wait for my confirmation”. ChatGPT proposes a model, length and resolution with its cost.
  4. Confirm. Say yes, or ask for a cheaper model or a shorter clip. ChatGPT returns the file and what it cost in coins.

For the whole process from the first prompt, see how to make a video with ChatGPT. On iPhone, the same account and coins work in the Dora app.

Questions people ask

How much does it cost to make a video with ChatGPT?

ChatGPT does not charge for it: the plugin that makes the video does. With Dora AI Video a video costs 15 to 76 coins at the default settings, about $0.15 to $1.52; the default model, Seedance 2 Mini, is 31 coins (about $0.31 to $0.62). Longer, sharper clips and sound cost more.

Does ChatGPT Plus pay for videos made with a plugin?

No. The video is made by the plugin's service and paid with your account there, not with your ChatGPT plan.

What is the cheapest video I can make from ChatGPT with Dora?

Grok Imagine 1.5 at 4 seconds and 480p, 10 coins, about $0.10 to $0.20.

How much is an image?

1 to 18 coins: Z-Image 1, Nano Banana 4, GPT Image 2 6, Nano Banana 2 (the default) 10 and Nano Banana Pro 18.

What happens if I have no coins?

Nothing is made and nothing is charged. ChatGPT tells you that generating needs coins and links to your balance and the plans.

Do I lose coins if a video fails?

No. If the generation fails, the coins go back to your balance.
