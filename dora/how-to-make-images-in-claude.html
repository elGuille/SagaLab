How to make images in Claude (2026)

Claude does not generate photos or illustrations on its own. Add an image connector and you can ask for them in a normal chat. Here is how, what it costs, and four images made that way, with the exact prompts.

Maruta G · 2 November 2026 · Leer en español

Short answer: Claude cannot make a photo or an illustration by itself; Anthropic's help center says so (Can Claude produce images?). It can draw diagrams and charts in code, and it can ask an image service to make a picture through a connector. This page uses Dora's connector, because we have checked it and can show what it made. The default image model, Nano Banana 2, costs about 10 coins an image, roughly $0.10 to $0.20, paid with Dora coins, not your Claude plan.

Before you start

  • A Claude plan with custom connectors. Anthropic lists Free, Pro, Max, Ultra, Team and Enterprise; Free is limited to one custom connector (Claude Help Center).
  • A Dora account with coins. You can create it when Claude asks you to sign in. Coins come from a plan or a pack (pricing).

Step by step

  1. Open the connector form. In Claude, go to Customize, then Connectors, click + Add and choose Add custom connector. The button on Dora's Claude page opens the same form and copies the address.
  2. Paste the address. Name it Dora, paste https://doravideo.com/mcp, click Continue, leave the sign-in settings as they are and click Add.
  3. Sign in. Sign in to Dora with Apple, Google or email. If you already use Dora, use the same account.
  4. Switch it on in a chat. In a chat, click +, open Connectors and check that Dora is on.
  5. Ask for the image. Describe it in normal words, with the shape you want (square, vertical or horizontal). Add “tell me the cost first and wait for my confirmation” if you want to approve it before it is made.

On Team and Enterprise plans an Owner adds the connector for the organisation and each member connects it. Full setup, with a button that opens the form: Dora for Claude; other assistants: Dora connectors.

What to ask for

  • Say what it is for. “An app icon”, “a background for an App Store screenshot with room for a headline at the top”, “a product shot from above”. The use decides the shape and the empty space.
  • Name the shape. Square for icons and posts, vertical 9:16 for stories and phone screens, horizontal for web headers.
  • Say what you do not want. “No text, no logos, no people” keeps stray letters and brand marks out.
  • Keep the details few. In our longest prompt below, three small details were lost.
  • Starting from your own photo: give Claude a public https link to it. Dora's guide tested links; attaching a photo to the chat was not tested there.

Without a connector

Let Claude draw it in code

Claude can build diagrams, charts and interactive visuals in HTML and SVG right in the chat, and adjust them when you ask (a beta on Claude web and desktop, per Anthropic). Ask for “an SVG of…” when you want a flowchart, a chart, a simple logo shape or a flat illustration you can edit. It will not look like a photo.

Let Claude write the prompt

Describe the image you want and ask Claude for a detailed prompt for the image tool you already use, with the shape, the light, what to leave out and where to keep empty space. Then paste it there. Prices are that tool's.

Videos too

The same connector makes videos; they come back as a link rather than inside the chat. The default video model is Seedance 2 Mini. Steps, real tests and costs: how to make videos in Claude. On iPhone, the same account and coins work in the Dora app.

Questions people ask

Can Claude make images?

Not by itself: Anthropic's help center says Claude does not generate photos or illustrations the way image tools do. It can draw diagrams and charts in HTML and SVG, and with an image connector such as Dora it can ask an image service to make a picture and show it in the chat.

Is there a Claude AI image generator?

Not built in. You add one as a connector under Customize, then Connectors, Add custom connector. With Dora the address is https://doravideo.com/mcp.

How much does an image in Claude cost?

Your Claude plan does not pay for it. With Dora the default image model, Nano Banana 2, is about 10 coins, roughly $0.10 to $0.20; the cheapest model is 1 coin.

Does it work on the free Claude plan?

Anthropic lists custom connectors on Free, limited to one. You still need coins in your Dora account.

Can Claude make an SVG?

Yes. Ask for an SVG and Claude writes it in the chat; it suits diagrams, charts, icons and flat shapes, not photos.

Can I edit my own photo?

Give Claude a public https link to the photo and say what to change. Dora's guide tested links; attaching a photo to the chat was not tested.
